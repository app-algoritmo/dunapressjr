## The World's First Consumer-Ready Humanoid Robot for Everyday Chores

In a world where technology increasingly intersects with daily life, the 1X NEO robot stands out as a groundbreaking innovation. Developed by 1X Technologies, a Palo Alto-based AI and robotics company, the 1X NEO robot is designed to automate household chores and provide personalized assistance. Launched on October 28, 2025, this humanoid robot promises to free up time for what truly matters by handling mundane tasks with efficiency and intelligence. As the first consumer-ready humanoid of its kind, the 1X NEO robot combines advanced AI with a safe, user-friendly design, making it an ideal companion for modern homes.

The journey of the 1X NEO robot began with 1X Technologies' commitment to creating safe humanoid robots. Backed by OpenAI, the company has focused on developing robots that can coexist harmoniously with humans. From its early prototypes like NEO Beta introduced in August 2024 and NEO Gamma in February 2025, to the full launch in late 2025, the 1X NEO robot represents years of research and iteration. This evolution highlights 1X's vertical integration approach, where everything from hardware components to AI models is built in-house to ensure affordability and safety.

## The Origins and Development of the 1X NEO Robot

1X Technologies, formerly known as Halodi Robotics, has been at the forefront of humanoid robotics since its inception. The company's mission is to produce quality robots quickly, emphasizing safety and practicality for home use. The 1X NEO robot builds on this foundation, incorporating lessons from previous models to create a bipedal robot that's lightweight, soft, and capable of natural interactions.

Key milestones include the introduction of NEO Beta in 2024, which showcased the robot's design for home environments, followed by NEO Gamma in early 2025, demonstrating improved capabilities like dish handling and navigation. By mid-2025, 1X unveiled Redwood, the AI powering the 1X NEO robot, a 160-million-parameter transformer model trained on vision, touch, and movement data from real-world teleoperation. This AI enables the 1X NEO robot to learn and adapt, marking a significant step toward general-purpose home assistance.

Collaborations, such as with NVIDIA's GEAR Lab, have further advanced the 1X NEO robot's abilities. In one project, the AI team spent time in homes teaching the robot tasks like doing dishes, gathering valuable real-world data. These efforts underscore 1X's data-driven approach to making the 1X NEO robot more intuitive and effective.

## Key Features of the 1X NEO Robot

The 1X NEO robot is packed with features that make it a versatile home helper. At its core is the Chores mode, allowing users to assign, schedule, and track tasks like folding laundry, tidying rooms, or organizing shelves. Users can interact via voice commands or a mobile app, with the robot operating autonomously for known tasks.

For unfamiliar chores, the Expert Mode lets owners schedule a remote 1X specialist to guide the 1X NEO robot, teaching it while completing the job. This teleoperation system is crucial for expanding capabilities, as the robot learns from these sessions and receives software updates to handle more complex activities, such as full laundry cycles or locating lost items.

Companion mode turns the 1X NEO robot into an intelligent assistant. It uses a built-in large language model (LLM) for natural conversations, providing advice, recipes, or even language lessons. Visual intelligence allows it to scan environments, like checking fridge contents to suggest meals, while memory features track reminders and progress.

Additional perks include serving as a mobile Bluetooth speaker, understanding gestures, and self-charging when low on battery. The 1X NEO robot's emotive ear rings signal status, adding a touch of personality.

## Technical Specifications of the 1X NEO Robot

Standing at 5 feet 6 inches and weighing just 66 pounds, the 1X NEO robot is designed for easy integration into homes. It can lift over 150 pounds and carry 55 pounds, with hands boasting 22 degrees of freedom for human-like dexterity.

The patented Tendon Drive system uses high-torque motors for gentle, compliant movements, ensuring safety. Its soft body, made from custom 3D lattice polymer, eliminates pinch points and reduces injury risk, with a Head Injury Criterion below 250.

Powered by the NEO Cortex chipset with NVIDIA Jetson Thor, it offers up to 2070 FP4 TFLOPS of AI compute. Sensors include dual 8.85MP stereo fisheye cameras, four beamforming microphones, and three speakers. Battery life is four hours, with quick charging adding an hour in six minutes.

Connectivity via WiFi, Bluetooth, and 5G supports remote control and updates. The robot operates quietly at 22 dB, quieter than a refrigerator.

## How the 1X NEO Robot Works

The 1X NEO robot defaults to autonomous operation using Redwood AI for tasks like navigation and basic chores. It responds to voice commands, distinguishing when addressed, thanks to audio intelligence.

For learning, teleoperation via Expert Mode collects data to improve the model, with updates enhancing autonomy over time. Owners control sessions to maintain privacy.

The app allows remote monitoring, chore management, and VR control. As it gains experience, the 1X NEO robot becomes more capable, adapting to unique home layouts.

## Pricing and Availability for the 1X NEO Robot

Pre-orders for the 1X NEO robot opened in October 2025, with early access at $20,000 for priority 2026 delivery, including premium support. A subscription option is $499 per month, shipping later. Initial deliveries focus on the US, expanding in 2027.

Colors include tan, gray, and dark brown, with accessories like a charger and case included.

## Safety and Privacy Considerations

Safety is paramount in the 1X NEO robot's design. Its soft, low-inertia structure and tendon drives prevent harm, making it suitable for families.

Privacy concerns arise from teleoperation, but 1X emphasizes user control over sessions and data use solely for improvements. Multiple security layers protect against misuse.

## Future Prospects for the 1X NEO Robot

As humanoid robotics advances, the 1X NEO robot is poised for growth. Ongoing updates will expand its skills, potentially into healthcare or education. 1X's focus on real-world training ensures continuous improvement.

Compared to competitors like Figure AI's robots for industrial use, the 1X NEO robot targets homes, prioritizing safety over heavy-duty tasks.

In conclusion, the 1X NEO robot represents a leap forward in home robotics, blending AI smarts with practical utility. As it enters homes in 2026, it could redefine how we manage daily life, making science fiction a reality.
