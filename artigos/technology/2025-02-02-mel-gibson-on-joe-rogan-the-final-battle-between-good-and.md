In a recent interview on *The Joe Rogan Experience* podcast, actor and filmmaker Mel Gibson shared his concerns about the direction of the Catholic Church, particularly regarding what he perceives as the introduction of practices that stray from Christian tradition. Gibson stated that "we have a pope who has brought the worship of false gods into the Vatican," referring to events such as the controversial inclusion of the Pachamama statue during the Amazon Synod in 2019. For him, such actions symbolize a spiritual battle between good and evil for the souls of humanity.

Gibson emphasized that this battle is about our souls, minds, and choices. He believes that evil seeks to destroy God's creation humanity and that we have been deceived for centuries. According to him, we are now in the "final stretch" of this spiritual struggle.

These statements reflect a worldview in which opposing spiritual forces are in constant conflict, influencing events and human decisions. Gibson suggests that it is crucial for individuals to seek the truth to free themselves from the evil influences that aim to lead them away from the divine path.

It is important to note that Gibson's views have sparked significant debate. Some critics argue that his perspectives may be influenced by conspiracy theories or personal interpretations of religious doctrine. For instance, the website *Catholic Answers* published an analysis titled *"The Hidden Danger of Mel Gibson on Joe Rogan"*, discussing the potential risks of such views for the unity of the Church.
