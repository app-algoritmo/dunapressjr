## **Drone Pilot Certifications in the European Union: Everything You Need to Know

Regulations for drone use have been evolving rapidly worldwide, and the European Union (EU) is no exception. To ensure safety and compliance, the European Union Aviation Safety Agency (EASA) has established clear guidelines for drone operations. In this context, certification provided by platforms such as Drone Class stands out as an essential step for those wishing to operate drones legally and responsibly within the EU.

### **What Certifications Are Available?

**Drone Class** offers courses and exams for two main types of certifications: A1-A3 and A2. Both are recognized by EASA and valid in all EU countries, as well as in Norway, Iceland, and Switzerland. Below, we explain each of them:

### **A1-A3 Certification

- **Who Needs It?** Pilots operating drones weighing between 250 grams and 25 kilograms in the open category.

- **Purpose:** To equip pilots to operate in sparsely populated areas while maintaining a safe distance from people.

- **Duration:** The course requires 6 to 10 hours of study, depending on the student’s pace.

- **Validity:** 5 years.

- **Cost:** €99 for the course and online exam, with an additional €20 fee for certificate issuance upon approval.

### **A2 Certification

- **Who Needs It?** Pilots wishing to operate drones with a C2 label in urban areas within the open category.

- **Purpose:** To allow operations in more densely populated areas, but without directly flying over people.

- **Duration:** Similar to A1-A3, between 6 and 10 hours of study.

- **Requirements:** Applicants must hold the A1-A3 certificate before starting the A2 course.

- **Validity:** 5 years.

- **Cost:** €99 for the course and exam, with a €20 fee for certificate issuance.

### **Are the Certifications Legal?

According to EASA regulations, certifications issued by any EU Member State are recognized across all other Member States. Drone Class, for example, is a platform registered in the Netherlands, ensuring the international validity of its certificates within the EU.

However, it is important to note that in addition to obtaining certifications, you must register as a drone operator with the aviation authority in your country of residence. Furthermore, you must comply with all local regulations related to drone usage.

### **Why Is Certification Important?

Certification is not just a legal requirement but also a guarantee that the pilot understands safety principles and best practices for drone use. This includes:

- Understanding airspace rules.

- Responsibility when interacting with other aircraft and the public.

- Reducing risks of accidents and damage.

### **How Does the Process Work?

1. **Enroll in the Course:** Access the platform, select the desired certification, and start the online course.

2. **Exam:** Take the exam directly on the platform, with unlimited attempts until approval.

3. **Certificate Issuance:** Pay the €20 fee to receive the official certificate.

If you plan to operate drones in the European Union, certification is a fundamental step. Beyond being a legal requirement, it demonstrates a commitment to safety and responsibility in using these technologies. Be sure to follow all steps and stay updated on local regulations to avoid penalties and ensure safe and efficient operations.
