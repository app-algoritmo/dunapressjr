## Canada Retaliates: Trudeau Announces 25% Tariffs on $155 Billion Worth of U.S. Products

In response to recent tariffs imposed by the United States, Canadian Prime Minister Justin Trudeau announced that Canada will implement retaliatory tariffs of 25% on $155 billion worth of U.S. products. The measure will take effect next Tuesday, with an initial phase covering $30 billion in U.S. imports, followed by an expansion to the remaining $125 billion after 21 days. ([apnews.com](https://apnews.com/article/753a09d56cd318f2eb1d2efe3c43b7d4?utm_source=chatgpt.com))

The affected products include beer, wine, bourbon, fruit juices, clothing, footwear, household appliances, wood products, and plastics. Additionally, the Canadian government is considering non-tariff measures, such as projects in the energy sector and critical mineral mining—both areas of essential cooperation between the two nations. ([apnews.com](https://apnews.com/article/753a09d56cd318f2eb1d2efe3c43b7d4?utm_source=chatgpt.com))

Trudeau emphasized the deep economic ties between Canada and the United States, stating, *"Our economies are deeply interconnected, and any action that separates us instead of bringing us together will cause harm on both sides of the border."* He also recalled historical moments of cooperation, such as Canada’s assistance during Hurricane Katrina and the California wildfires, affirming, *"We have always stood by the American people, mourning together."* ([apnews.com](https://apnews.com/article/753a09d56cd318f2eb1d2efe3c43b7d4?utm_source=chatgpt.com))

The decision to impose retaliatory tariffs comes after U.S. President Donald Trump announced 25% tariffs on Canadian and Mexican imports and 10% tariffs on Chinese goods, justifying them as measures to protect American interests and reduce illegal immigration and drug trafficking. Analysts warn that if these tariffs remain in place, Canada could enter a recession within six months. ([apnews.com](https://apnews.com/article/753a09d56cd318f2eb1d2efe3c43b7d4?utm_source=chatgpt.com))

Trudeau concluded his statement with a call for national unity and support for Canadian-made products, stressing that Canada is prepared to defend its interests and its people while striving to maintain a fair and stable trade relationship with its southern neighbor. *"If President Trump wants a new golden era for the United States, the best path forward is to work with Canada, not punish us,"* he declared.
