The recent discussion between President Donald Trump and Volodymyr Zelenskyy has revealed, once and for all, the true face of the Ukrainian leader: an opportunist who plays with global security and manipulates allies to gain political and military advantages. The farce was exposed on live television when Zelenskyy attempted to blackmail the United States by demanding ground troops in exchange for a previously negotiated mining deal. With this move, he jeopardized his own credibility and demonstrated that he is not seeking peace but rather prolonging a conflict at the expense of Western allies.

## **Rubio Deceived: The Failure of Diplomatic Theater

Senator Marco Rubio was confident that the mineral agreement between the U.S. and Ukraine was secured. Zelenskyy assured him that everything was settled, leading Rubio to promise President Trump that the deal was done. However, at the last minute, Zelenskyy changed the terms, demanding that the United States send ground troops to Ukraine. This premeditated move was calculated to create a deadlock, as Zelenskyy knew perfectly well that Trump would never agree to such a demand. The Ukrainian leader did not act as an honest partner but as a manipulator willing to risk everything to prolong the war and maintain his position of power.

## **The Farce Collapses: Trump Cancels the Deal

Trump’s response was immediate and forceful. During the historic meeting at the White House, the American president made it clear that Zelenskyy is not truly committed to peace. Trump publicly stated that Ukraine does not hold the upper hand and that without the U.S., the country’s position is weak. Moreover, Trump exposed Zelenskyy’s lack of gratitude for the massive investments Washington has made to sustain Ukraine’s defense. “If you didn’t have our military equipment, this war would have ended in two weeks,” Trump declared, making it clear that Ukraine’s future depends entirely on the goodwill of the United States.

The discussion escalated when Vice President JD Vance confronted Zelenskyy, stating that Ukraine does not have enough manpower to continue fighting without external support. The Ukrainian leader reacted aggressively, insisting that Vance should visit the country to understand the real situation. This attempt to deflect only confirmed Zelenskyy’s desperation and his strategy of keeping the conflict going indefinitely to continue receiving external aid.

## **Zelenskyy Disrespects the U.S. and Faces Immediate Consequences

During the discussion, Trump did not hold back and interrupted Zelenskyy to remind him of the massive aid the U.S. has already provided. “You need to be more grateful. You don’t have the cards. With us, you have the cards. Without us, you have no cards at all,” Trump stated, highlighting Ukraine’s total dependence on the United States.

The public clash ended humiliatingly for Zelenskyy, who saw his press conference canceled and had to leave the White House abruptly. The crisis deepened when House Speaker Mike Johnson demanded the resignation of the Ukrainian ambassador to the U.S., Oksana Markarova, accusing her of political interference by organizing Zelenskyy’s visits as a partisan campaign event in favor of the Democrats.

## **Zelenskyy Is Not Trustworthy

The illusion that Zelenskyy is an honest leader committed to peace has been completely shattered. He has revealed himself as an opportunist who manipulates allies, shifts positions as convenient, and plays with global stability to gain personal benefits. His confrontation with Trump proved that he is not interested in real negotiations but rather in prolonging the conflict to maintain his political relevance and secure continued Western financial and military support.

Trump made it clear: Zelenskyy can return to the negotiating table when he is ready for peace. Until then, he remains just another desperate politician gambling with the world's security.
