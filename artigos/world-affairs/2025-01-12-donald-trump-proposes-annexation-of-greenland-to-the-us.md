Former US President Donald Trump has reignited an old controversy by once again proposing the annexation of Greenland to the United States. The idea, which had been floated during his presidency, is once again drawing global attention and sparking reactions both internationally and within the US.

## **What’s Behind the Proposal?

Greenland, an autonomous territory under Denmark’s administration, is known for its vast mineral reserves, strategic position in the Arctic, and growing geopolitical importance due to melting ice caps, which could open new shipping routes and facilitate access to natural resources. For Trump, annexing the island would be a strategic move to secure US supremacy in the region and expand its economic and military influence.

In recent statements, Trump claimed that Greenland represents "enormous potential" and that its acquisition would benefit both the United States and the island's residents. He suggested that the US could invest in infrastructure, healthcare, and education in the region, offering local inhabitants economic and social advantages that Denmark might not be able to match.

## **International Reactions

The proposal was met with immediate rejection by the Danish government, which reaffirmed that Greenland is not for sale. Denmark’s Prime Minister called the suggestion "absurd" and reiterated the country’s commitment to the autonomy and interests of the Greenlandic people.

Greenland’s leaders also responded, emphasizing the island’s autonomy and stating that any decision about its political future must be made by its people. The Greenlandic Parliament issued a statement asserting that the territory "is not for sale" and reaffirmed the island’s focus on independence as a top priority.

## **Impact on US Politics

Within the United States, the idea sparked mixed reactions. While some of Trump’s supporters praised the proposal as bold and visionary, critics dismissed it as a political distraction or an attempt to reinforce a narrative of global territorial dominance.

Analysts have also suggested that the proposal may be a strategy to divert attention from domestic issues or a way for Trump to maintain his presence on the international political stage.

## **Feasibility of the Proposal

The annexation of Greenland faces significant diplomatic and logistical challenges. In addition to strong opposition from Denmark and Greenland, there are legal and ethical questions surrounding territorial sovereignty. International law experts argue that such an acquisition would require unanimous consent from all parties involved, which seems unlikely in the current context.

Moreover, the financial cost of developing and integrating Greenland into US infrastructure would be astronomical, especially given the climatic and logistical challenges of managing such a vast and inhospitable territory.

Although Trump’s proposal to annex Greenland has resurfaced as a controversial topic, political and diplomatic realities suggest that it is unlikely to materialize. However, the discussion highlights the growing strategic importance of the Arctic and underscores the interest of major powers in asserting influence in the region.

Meanwhile, the people of Greenland remain at the center of this geopolitical debate, emphasizing their right to self-determination and their desire to shape their own future.
