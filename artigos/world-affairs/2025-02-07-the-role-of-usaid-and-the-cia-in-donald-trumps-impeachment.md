The recent shutdown of USAID by former President Donald Trump has brought to light new revelations about the agency's role and that of other government entities in influencing political processes, both abroad and within the United States. Investigations reveal that the CIA and USAID played a significant role in Trump's 2019 impeachment, raising questions about the legality and legitimacy of this intervention.

## **USAID: A Development Agency or a Regime Change Instrument?

USAID's defenders argue that its purpose is to provide humanitarian aid and foster development in poor countries. However, evidence suggests that the agency operates as a regime change engine, funding and supporting groups that favor the interests of the U.S. government. This modus operandi seems not to be limited to foreign nations but also extends to U.S. domestic politics.

## **The Role of OCCRP and USAID’s Influence

During Trump’s impeachment process, a key element was a complaint filed by a CIA analyst, originally recruited by the Obama administration, who alleged that Trump had pressured Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky to investigate Joe Biden and his son, Hunter Biden. This accusation was based on a report published by the Organized Crime and Corruption Reporting Project (OCCRP), an investigative journalism organization that received substantial funding from USAID.

The OCCRP report was cited four times in the complaint, serving as the foundation for the narrative that led to the formal accusation against Trump. However, a 2024 investigation conducted by multiple European news networks revealed that USAID exercised direct control over OCCRP, approving its work plans and hiring key personnel. This indicates that the organization may not be as independent as it claims to be.

## **The Connection Between the CIA, USAID, and Impeachment

The investigation also revealed that OCCRP was not limited to producing investigative reports but actively engaged in political influence, promoting government changes in various countries. Drew Sullivan, OCCRP’s co-founder, stated that his organization was involved in replacing governments in at least five or six countries and investigating prime ministers who were eventually removed from office.

If the CIA and USAID used OCCRP to topple foreign governments and the same method was applied against Trump, this constitutes an illegal operation within the United States. It is illegal and potentially treasonous for intelligence agencies and their intermediaries to interfere in U.S. domestic politics.

## **Censorship and Retaliation

The investigation also uncovered censorship attempts. A documentary by German broadcaster NDR, in partnership with other European and U.S. media outlets, exposed the ties between USAID and OCCRP. However, the broadcaster was pressured to withhold the material after OCCRP co-founder Drew Sullivan intervened directly.

Moreover, OCCRP threatened to sue Public after the news outlet raised questions about its ties with USAID. In an email, OCCRP’s editor-in-chief, Miranda Patrucic, claimed that the allegations were false and defamatory. However, neither OCCRP nor any other entity managed to refute the evidence presented by journalistic investigations.

## **An Internal Coup?

Donald Trump's 2019 impeachment was presented as a legal process conducted by Congress. However, evidence suggests that it may have been the result of a regime change operation led by the CIA and USAID, using OCCRP as an intermediary.

If confirmed, this raises serious questions about the integrity of the U.S. democratic system and the influence of intelligence agencies in domestic political processes. Trump's closure of USAID may have been an attempt to cut off these operations' influence, but the impact of this revelation may resonate for years, fueling debates about the true autonomy of the executive branch and the threats to the U.S. democratic process.
