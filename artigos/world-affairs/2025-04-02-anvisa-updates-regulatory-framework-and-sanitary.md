In recent years, Brazil has made significant progress in aligning its health regulations with international standards, strengthening its global position in import and export compliance. The Brazilian Health Regulatory Agency (ANVISA) has played a key role in this process, updating its regulatory framework across several product categories.

Since 2022, **hundreds of new regulations** have been issued to reflect global standards and best regulatory practices adopted by agencies such as the **EMA (Europe), FDA (USA),** and **PMDA (Japan)**. Many of these regulations came into effect in **2025**, significantly impacting pharmaceutical operations and related industries.

Key affected areas include:

- **Medicines:** electronic submission requirements, clinical evaluation, and biosimilar protocols

- **Medical Devices / SAMD / IVD:** new risk classifications, safety/performance evaluations, and alignment with the EU MDR

- **Cosmetics:** updated risk classes, mandatory labeling, and safety testing

- **Food:** revised nutrition labeling and regulatory treatment of functional ingredients

- **CMED / Price Registration:** updated requirements for drug pricing and international comparability

To support professionals in navigating these changes, **Sanitary Intelligence**, a leading training provider for over 15 years, announces its **2025 Regulatory Update Course Edition**, featuring **live Saturday classes** to accommodate full-time professionals.
