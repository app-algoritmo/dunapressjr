### **From a symbol of trust to an example of neglect: What happened to Toyota?

Toyota was once synonymous with **quality, commitment, and respect for consumers**. In the past, the Japanese brand valued **compliance and ensured customer satisfaction was a priority**. However, this reputation is being **destroyed by disgraceful behavior in Europe**: **neglect, misleading advertising, and abusive demands that violate laws and best business practices**.

Consumers who buy Toyota cars in Europe are being treated **with total disrespect**, especially in **Spain**, where **authorized dealerships such as Nipocar/Louzao and Grupo Breogan, in Galicia,** are responsible for **absurd, illegal, and discriminatory situations**.

## **THE SCAM OF THE "INTERNATIONAL MAINTENANCE PLAN" THAT DOESN’T EXIST

Toyota markets **maintenance plans supposedly valid throughout Europe**, but when customers try to use them outside the country of purchase, they **discover they were deceived**.

The abuses include:

❌ **Misleading sales tactics** – Customers pay, believing the service will be accepted across Europe, only to be informed later that they **are not entitled to the service**.

❌ **Impossible refunds** – When consumers demand their money back, Toyota imposes ridiculous barriers, such as requiring them **to travel 3,000 km** to present documents that **could be sent digitally**—especially considering that **in today’s digital world, even invoices are electronic!**

❌ **Total disregard for the consumer** – The company **simply ignores support requests** and avoids responding to complaints.

This is **not an isolated mistake**. At authorized dealerships such as **Nipocar/Louzao and Grupo Breogan, in Galicia, Spain**, consumers report **practices that completely violate compliance principles and customer respect**.

## **DISCRIMINATORY REQUIREMENTS: NEGLECT AND PREJUDICE

The behavior of these dealerships goes beyond mere negligence. Consumer reports reveal that **the requirements imposed for services and refunds are not only abusive but also discriminatory**.

🔴 **Gender discrimination** – A female customer, despite having sufficient income to pay for the service, was **forced by the dealership to present a man's income statement for her request to be accepted!** An **illegal and unacceptable** demand in the 21st century!

🔴 **Absurd bureaucratic barriers** – Toyota **forces customers to travel thousands of kilometers** just to present a **digital invoice ON PAPER**, completely disregarding **the digital era** and the recognition of electronic documents by **tax authorities themselves**.

🔴 **Clear intent to prevent refunds** – The company **creates artificial difficulties** so that consumers either **give up on their rights** or have their claims **denied**.

## **TOYOTA EUROPE: A SHOCKING LEVEL OF DISREGARD

The consumer in question **contacted Toyota customer service in both Spain and Belgium (Toyota Europe)**, expecting a resolution. Instead, **she was met with an outrageous level of neglect and disrespect**.

Despite providing **all legally valid and required documentation**, Toyota **insisted on making unrealistic demands to avoid processing the refund**.

➡️ **They forced the customer to go "in person" to the dealership where the car was purchased**—an unreasonable demand that would require her to **travel 3,000 km** just to be heard.

➡️ **This demand is completely absurd and abusive**, proving that Toyota is **deliberately creating obstacles** to **avoid fulfilling its obligations**.

These practices not only **violate the European Consumer Protection Code** but also **contradict the very values Toyota once claimed to uphold**.

## **TOYOTA IS VIOLATING EUROPEAN LEGISLATION

The European Union has **clear laws** to protect consumers from abuses like these, and Toyota is violating several directives, including:

⚖️ **Directive 2011/83/EU on Consumer Rights** – Ensures that service information must be **clear** and that refunds **must be accessible** without abusive barriers.

⚖️ **Directive 2005/29/EC on Unfair Commercial Practices** – Explicitly prohibits **misleading advertising** and omitting information that deceives consumers.

⚖️ **Regulation (EU) 2017/2394 on Consumer Protection Cooperation** – Guarantees that companies operating in multiple EU countries **must respect consumer rights across borders**.
