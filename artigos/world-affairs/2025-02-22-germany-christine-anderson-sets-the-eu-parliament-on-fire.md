German MEP **Christine Anderson** once again shook the European Parliament with a fiery speech, calling for a radical shift away from globalist policies and drawing inspiration from **Donald Trump’s leadership**. With sharp words and a firm stance, she made a direct appeal to dismantle supranational structures that, according to her, undermine national sovereignty and the individual rights of European citizens.

## **"Trump is showing us the way"

In her address, Anderson emphasized that Europe must learn from the United States under Trump’s leadership. She warned of the dangers of following the agenda of globalist elites and urged European lawmakers to abandon policies that, in her view, only destroy economies, erode freedoms, and reinforce centralized control in the hands of a few.

*"Donald Trump is showing us the way. And unless we want to go down in history as complete idiots, we should follow his lead!"*, she declared, receiving applause from allies and uneasy glances from opponents.

## **Attacking the Green Deal and Global Organizations

Anderson did not hold back in criticizing the **Green Deal**, calling it a scheme designed to destroy industry and subject Europe to an unrealistic and unsustainable ecological agenda. She also attacked organizations such as **USAID (United States Agency for International Development)**, the **World Health Organization (WHO)**, and other global institutions, accusing them of serving obscure interests aimed at weakening national autonomy.

*"Tear down the Green Deal, dismantle USAID, cut ties with the WHO! It must all collapse! We do not need these elitist bureaucrats telling us how to live!"*, she fired back.

## **A New Economic and Decentralized Approach

Beyond rejecting the globalist model, Anderson advocated for an alternative based on financial freedom and economic decentralization—aligned with Elon Musk’s vision of a monetary system that is more accessible, dynamic, and independent from government control.

*"We need a system that truly empowers people, one that promotes freedom and innovation instead of trapping them in bureaucratic networks and oppressive regulations."*

This idea aligns with a growing movement for financial independence driven by technology and innovation, where transactions are fast, accessible, and free from artificial barriers imposed by central banks and large corporations.

## **Christine Anderson and the Conservative Wave in Europe

Anderson’s stance reflects a growing movement in Europe that seeks inspiration from Trump and other sovereignist leaders worldwide. Rising dissatisfaction with EU policies—particularly regarding the economy, immigration, and excessive regulation—has propelled politicians like her, who are unafraid to challenge the establishment.

As the European elections approach, speeches like Anderson’s are gaining traction. Will Europe finally follow Trump’s path? According to Christine Anderson, this is the only way to prevent the EU from sinking further into a future of servitude and economic decline.

The battle is just beginning.
