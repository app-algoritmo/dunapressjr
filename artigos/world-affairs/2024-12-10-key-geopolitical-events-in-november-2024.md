## Trump’s Visit to France, Global Conflicts, and Emerging Powers

In November 2024, the global geopolitical landscape was marked by significant events that shaped international relations and influenced global dynamics. Among the most notable occurrences was the visit of U.S. President-elect Donald Trump to France, alongside other impactful events.

## **Donald Trump’s Victory in the U.S. Presidential Election

On November 5, Donald Trump was elected to a non-consecutive second term as President of the United States, with JD Vance as his Vice President. This victory marks the first time since Grover Cleveland in 1892 that a president has returned to office after an interval. Trump’s election signals potential changes in U.S. domestic and foreign policies, creating expectations and uncertainties in the international arena.

## **Trump’s Visit to France and the Reopening of Notre-Dame Cathedral

In early December, Donald Trump embarked on his first international trip after the election, attending the reopening ceremony of Notre-Dame Cathedral in Paris on December 7. The event was attended by around 50 heads of state and government, including French President Emmanuel Macron and Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky. The restoration of the cathedral, devastated by a fire in 2019, involved over 2,000 workers and 250 companies, being hailed as the “project of the century.” During his visit, Trump discussed geopolitical issues such as the war in Ukraine and called for an "immediate ceasefire" between Ukraine and Russia, urging leaders to establish conditions for negotiations.

## **Conflicts in the Middle East and Ukraine

In November, conflicts in the Middle East and Ukraine continued to dominate international attention. The war in Ukraine persisted without signs of imminent resolution, while tensions in the Middle East between Israel and Palestinian groups like Hamas resulted in clashes and regional instability. These conflicts sparked debates in international forums such as the G20 Summit in Brazil, where leaders discussed the impacts of these crises on global order.

## **G20 Summit in Brazil

On November 18-19, Rio de Janeiro hosted the G20 Summit, bringing together leaders of the world's largest economies to discuss topics such as trade, climate change, and international security. The summit took place amidst heightened geopolitical tensions influenced by Donald Trump’s return to the U.S. presidency and ongoing conflicts in Gaza and Ukraine. Chinese President Xi Jinping played a central role in discussions, reflecting China’s growing influence on the global stage.

## **India’s Rise as a Global Power

In 2024, India solidified its status as a significant political and economic power, not specifically aligned with any major power or bloc. Its growing influence on the international agenda underscores the emerging multipolarity in the geopolitical landscape, where countries like India, Saudi Arabia, Turkey, South Africa, and Brazil play increasingly prominent roles.

These events reflect the complexity and interconnectedness of international relations in 2024, emphasizing the need for cooperation and dialogue to address global challenges.
