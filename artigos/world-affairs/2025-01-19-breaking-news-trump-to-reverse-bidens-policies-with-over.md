## 🚨 I 🇺🇸 TRUMP PREPARES A WAVE OF EXECUTIVE ORDERS TO REVERSE BIDEN'S POLICIES FROM DAY ONE

In an announcement already making waves across the United States and the global political stage, Donald Trump revealed his plan to issue over 100 executive orders on his first day back in the White House. His goal is clear: to undo all of Joe Biden’s key measures, mirroring Biden’s approach at the start of his term, where he reversed Trump-era policies.

## **A Strategic and Decisive Move

This declaration underscores Trump’s intention to immediately set a new tone of governance and break with his predecessor. According to the former president, these executive orders will address areas such as immigration, energy, trade, and education — sectors where Biden’s policies have sparked controversy.

## **What Can Be Expected?

- **Border Policies**: The reinstatement of the *"Remain in Mexico"* program and strengthening of the border wall construction are highly anticipated.

- **Energy and Environment**: Trump is expected to reverse restrictions on the energy industry and revive projects like the Keystone XL pipeline.

- **Educational Reforms**: These could include the return of merit-based teaching programs and limits on progressive theories in school curricula.

- **International Relations**: A reconfiguration of trade agreements and multilateral treaties, prioritizing the "America First" strategy, is likely.

## **A Historical Trend

Using executive orders to dismantle policies from the previous administration is not new. Biden began his presidency with over 40 executive orders in his first week, a move Trump now promises to surpass. This highlights the growing polarization in American politics, where each new administration seems focused on undoing the work of its predecessor.

## **Polarized Reactions

While Trump supporters celebrate this strategy as a return to conservative values, critics warn about the instability caused by this policy alternation every four years. Moreover, the extensive use of executive orders could face legal and political challenges in Congress and the courts.

With this promise, Trump aims to solidify his leadership among Republicans and demonstrate to his voters that he is ready to act immediately. The remaining question is how the political system will respond and whether these measures will address the deep challenges the United States faces or perpetuate polarization instead.

The battle for the nation’s future has just begun, and the first round promises to be one of the most intense in modern history.
