## **Trump and Putin Discuss Cooperation and the War in Ukraine: A New Path to Peace?

In a recent post, former U.S. President Donald J. Trump revealed details of an extensive and productive conversation with Russian President Vladimir Putin. The discussion covered a range of strategic topics, including the war in Ukraine, energy issues, artificial intelligence, and the stability of the dollar.

Trump emphasized the historical significance of U.S.-Russia cooperation, recalling their alliance in World War II and the significant losses both nations suffered in that conflict. According to him, both he and Putin agreed on the urgent need to stop the ongoing deaths in the Russia-Ukraine war.

### **Start of Negotiations and Contact with Zelenskyy

The former president announced that the first steps toward peace negotiations will begin immediately, starting with direct contact with Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy. Trump stated that he will personally lead this process and has designated a high-level team to conduct the negotiations, including:

- **Marco Rubio** – Secretary of State

- **John Ratcliffe** – CIA Director

- **Michael Waltz** – National Security Advisor

- **Steve Witkoff** – Ambassador and Special Envoy

Trump expressed confidence in the success of these negotiations, emphasizing that "millions of people have died in a war that would not have happened" if he were president. However, he acknowledged that the conflict has already taken place and must now come to an end.

### **Putin Adopts Trump's Slogan: "Common Sense"

One of the most notable aspects of the conversation was Trump's mention that Putin used his campaign slogan, **"Common Sense."** For Trump, this indicates an alignment in strategic visions and reinforces the possibility of a more pragmatic dialogue between the two nations.

### **Thanking Putin for the Release of Marc Fogel

Beyond geopolitical issues, Trump took the opportunity to thank Putin for the release of Marc Fogel, an American citizen who had been detained in Russia. He mentioned that Fogel was personally welcomed at the White House and that this event is a positive sign for future diplomatic negotiations.

### **Conclusion: Is the End of the War Near?

The conversation between Trump and Putin raises crucial questions about the future of the war in Ukraine. If Trump indeed manages to broker a ceasefire, it could mark a historic moment in global diplomacy. However, it remains to be seen how Zelenskyy and Ukraine’s allies will react to this initiative.

Meanwhile, the world eagerly awaits further developments in these negotiations and their potential impact on international politics and global security.
