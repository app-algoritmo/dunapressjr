The newly appointed U.S. Secretary of Energy, Chris Wright, has brought to light one of the biggest controversies in modern politics: the true cost and effectiveness of the climate policies imposed by the political and media elite. In an official statement released by the White House, Wright revealed that the measures supposedly taken to "save the planet" have actually served to enrich bureaucrats and globalist corporations at the expense of ordinary citizens.

## **$2 Trillion for a Minimal Reduction in Fossil Fuel Use

According to Wright, the United States has spent **over $2 trillion** on policies aimed at reducing fossil fuel use, resulting in only a **2% decrease in consumption**. This staggering figure exposes an uncomfortable truth: the climate agenda, far from being about environmental preservation, has become a massive wealth redistribution scheme, siphoning trillions of dollars from the productive sector and the middle class to subsidize “green solutions” that often fail to deliver tangible results.

“The media and politicians **never** bothered to actually learn the science behind climate change,” Wright said. He harshly criticized climate alarmism and the narratives built around flawed computer models, which repeatedly fail to predict real-world scenarios.

## **Reorienting America’s Energy Policy

In light of this reality, the Trump administration is adopting a **pragmatic approach**: instead of prioritizing net-zero emissions goals, the new Secretary of Energy aims to strengthen the **production of affordable, reliable, and abundant energy**.

The new directive includes:

- **Expanding oil and natural gas production** to ensure energy security and lower prices for consumers.

- **Investing in modernizing energy infrastructure**, making it more resilient to crises and extreme events.

- **Reevaluating subsidies for renewable energy** that lack proven efficiency, redirecting funds toward truly promising technologies.

- **Expanding nuclear energy**, recognized as a clean, efficient, and sustainable long-term energy source.

“Our goal is not to please bureaucrats at international conferences but to ensure that American energy remains cheap, accessible, and secure for everyone,” Wright stated.

## **The Truth About the Climate Agenda

Since the climate agenda was integrated into public policy, billions of dollars have been funneled into research and projects that have failed to produce real solutions. Meanwhile, the world's largest polluters—China and India—continue increasing their emissions without facing the same regulatory pressures imposed on the West. Wright asks: **if the real concern was the environment, why aren’t these nations held to the same standards?**

Wright’s revelations confirm what many have long suspected: **the "fight against climate change" is not about saving the planet but about wealth transfer and control by globalist elites**. With this new approach, the U.S. reaffirms its commitment to energy independence and the prosperity of its citizens, rather than submitting to policies that only drain resources and limit economic growth.

The appointment of Chris Wright to the Department of Energy marks a **turning point** in America’s energy policy. With a vision focused on **efficiency, innovation, and energy independence**, the United States is moving away from the trap of ineffective climate policies and back on the path of sustainable and responsible growth.

The real question now is: **how many other countries will have the courage to follow this path and expose the great climate scam?**
