Former Brazilian volleyball player Ana Paula Henkel recently expressed her support for the executive order signed by U.S. President Donald Trump, titled **"Keeping Men Out of Women's Sports."** This measure aims to prohibit transgender women from participating in female sports competitions, arguing that it preserves the integrity and fairness of women's sports.

On her social media, Ana Paula celebrated the decision, stating that *"the war against women's sports is over"* and that *"women's sports will be for women only."* She emphasized that, for the past decade, she has been fighting alongside other women to protect female spaces in sports, restrooms, locker rooms, and schools. She underscored the importance of defending truth and biological reality, criticizing agreements that, in her view, fail to protect these principles.

Trump's executive order, signed on **National Girls and Women in Sports Day**, mandates that educational institutions allowing transgender athletes to compete in female sports teams will be in violation of **Title IX**, a federal law prohibiting sex-based discrimination in federally funded educational programs. As a result, these institutions may lose federal funding if they do not comply with the new regulations.

Ana Paula Henkel, who was inducted into the **Volleyball Hall of Fame in October 2024**, has been a vocal advocate in debates surrounding transgender participation in women's sports. In her **Hall of Fame acceptance speech**, she addressed issues related to protecting female spaces and ensuring fairness in sports competitions.

Trump’s decision has sparked mixed reactions. While some support the measure as a way to uphold fairness in women’s sports, others criticize it as discriminatory against transgender individuals. Civil rights organizations and LGBTQ+ advocacy groups have condemned the order, arguing that it violates the rights of transgender students and promotes discrimination.

Ana Paula Henkel continues to stand firm in her position, expressing gratitude to women who, according to her, never accepted the *“spiral of silence”* and have always fought to protect women’s spaces in sports. She concluded her message with the phrase:

## "Make Women’s Sports Great Again!"
