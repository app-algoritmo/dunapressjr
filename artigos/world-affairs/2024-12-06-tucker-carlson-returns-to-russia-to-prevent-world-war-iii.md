In the week since leaving Russia, where we conducted a pivotal interview with Vladimir Putin, we’ve observed a startling escalation in tensions between the United States and Russia. The Biden Administration’s actions have brought the two nations perilously close to a nuclear confrontation with the country that possesses the world’s largest nuclear arsenal. In the wake of Donald Trump’s election as president-elect, the situation has intensified dramatically. American military strikes on Russian soil, resulting in the deaths of Russian soldiers, have propelled this undeclared conflict into what can only be described as a "hot war" — one that Americans neither voted for nor fully understand. Alarmingly, there is no active diplomatic backchannel between Washington and Moscow, leaving the world teetering closer to nuclear annihilation than during the Cuban Missile Crisis. With no one seemingly working to prevent catastrophe, our return to Moscow yesterday was driven by a singular mission: to interview Russia’s Foreign Minister Sergey Lavrov and seek answers to the pressing question — can this trajectory be reversed? - *Tucker Carlson*

In a world increasingly on edge, where global tensions between major powers threaten to spiral out of control, journalist Tucker Carlson has returned to Russia on a personal mission: to prevent World War III.

Speaking from Moscow, Carlson shared his purpose: “We came back to Moscow yesterday to interview the Foreign Minister of Russia... To ask: Are we headed toward an unprecedented conflict between Russia and the United States? Is there any way to peel Russia back from the East, from the sphere of China, back to the West?"

Carlson’s initiative is both timely and bold. With geopolitical fault lines deepening, his pursuit of dialogue underscores the vital need for peacemakers in uncertain times. His actions echo the words, “Blessed are the peacemakers, for they will be called children of God.”

This mission is not just about politics; it is a call for humanity to step back from the brink. Carlson’s efforts highlight the importance of diplomacy and the courage to ask difficult questions in pursuit of solutions.

As the world watches, let us hope that his work fosters understanding and averts the unthinkable. GOD BLESS & STRENGTHEN HIM.
