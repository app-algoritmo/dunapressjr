The possibility of the Panama Canal returning to U.S. control has gained attention following recent statements by former President Donald Trump. He reiterated the strategic importance of the canal to American interests, calling it "unacceptable" for such a vital asset to remain outside the direct control of the United States, hinting at potential measures to regain influence in the region.

!imagem

## **The History of the Panama Canal

The Panama Canal is a landmark of global engineering. Built by the United States and inaugurated in 1914, the canal was under American administration until 1999, when it was handed over to the Panamanian government under the Torrijos-Carter Treaties. This transfer marked a milestone in Panama’s sovereignty, but the canal has remained at the center of geopolitical disputes ever since.

## **Why Is the Panama Canal So Strategic?

The canal connects the Atlantic and Pacific Oceans, drastically reducing maritime transport time and global shipping costs. Its strategic location makes it a point of economic and military interest. Currently, China’s growing influence in the region through trade agreements and investments has raised concerns in the U.S., which views this as a threat to the balance of power.

## **Trump's Statements

Trump emphasized that the canal is "essential to national security" and should never have been handed over to Panama. He also criticized China’s increasing presence in global maritime trade, suggesting that under his leadership, measures would be taken to "regain control of strategic assets."

These remarks reignite debates about the canal’s future and the potential impact of U.S. intervention in the region. While no concrete measures have been announced, Trump’s position reopens discussions on sovereignty, global trade, and geopolitics.

## **What Does This Mean for Latin America?

A potential U.S. attempt to regain control of the canal could lead to diplomatic tensions with Latin American countries, which have historically resisted American interference in the region. Moreover, the canal is a significant source of revenue for Panama, playing a crucial role in its economy.

## **The Current Scenario

So far, the Panamanian government has not commented on Trump’s statements, but resistance to such an idea is expected. Experts argue that any attempt at military or economic control of the canal could trigger international reactions, particularly from China, which also has strategic interests in the region.

The future of the Panama Canal remains uncertain, but it is clear that it continues to be a central piece in the geopolitical chessboard. Only time will tell how tensions will unfold in the coming months and whether Trump’s words will translate into concrete actions.
