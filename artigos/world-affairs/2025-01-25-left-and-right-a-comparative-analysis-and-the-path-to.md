Global politics remains divided between two poles that shape the course of nations: the left and the right. While the left is known for its broad social policies and greater state intervention, the right advocates for individual freedom, entrepreneurship, and freer markets. However, the disunity within the right has been a significant obstacle to its effectiveness, often resulting in missed opportunities to reverse the chaotic scenarios created by the left.

## **The Chaos Created by the Left and the Right's Failure to Act

The last decade has been marked by chaotic situations driven by leftist governments, including high unemployment rates, economic instability, and social tensions. Despite these challenges, the right has failed to capitalize on these circumstances to implement structural changes. The main reason? Its lack of unity.

A notable example was the 2022 U.S. presidential election. With the left united around its agenda, the fragmented right, lacking a cohesive front, failed to secure Donald Trump’s reelection. This defeat not only delayed the development of the United States but also caused global tensions and increased unemployment, exacerbating economic chaos.

Had the right demonstrated the same unity as the left, the outcome could have been different, bringing stability and prosperity at a critical time. This episode serves as a stark reminder of the importance of working together to prevent crises from deepening.

## **What the Right Can Learn from the Left

Despite its limitations, the left is highly effective in uniting around a common agenda, even when internal disagreements exist. This cohesion is a lesson the right urgently needs to learn. While the right takes pride in its advocacy for individual freedom and private initiative, these same characteristics often hinder the formation of alliances and unified strategies.

The right has had numerous opportunities to reverse adverse scenarios, but its inability to act in a coordinated manner has only amplified the difficulties faced by the societies it seeks to represent.

## **Supporting Small Businesses and Independent Professionals

To overcome its limitations and rebuild public trust, the right must invest in policies that directly benefit small businesses and independent professionals. These sectors are crucial for job creation and the strengthening of local economies.

Measures such as reducing bureaucracy, offering tax incentives, and facilitating access to credit can turn entrepreneurship into a powerful tool for economic recovery. Furthermore, by prioritizing these groups, the right can demonstrate its commitment to improving people’s lives and not just defending large economic interests.

## **Strengthening the Entire Productive Chain

Another essential priority is strengthening the entire productive chain. This means investing in infrastructure, technological innovation, and technical education, ensuring that every sector – from small producers to large industries – operates efficiently. Without this integrated support, any attempt at recovery will be superficial and unsustainable.

The comparison between left and right goes beyond ideologies; it’s about identifying the lessons each political spectrum can teach. The chaos caused by the left in the last decade has made it clear that the right has a crucial role to play in reversing this situation, but to do so, it must overcome its disunity.

The 2022 U.S. presidential election is a clear example of how a lack of cohesion can have global consequences. The right must learn from the left's ability to unite around common goals. Only with this unity will it be possible to turn proposals into concrete actions, promote prosperity, and prevent crises like the ones we are experiencing today from persisting.
