In a historic move that is already reverberating across the global stage, Argentina's President **Javier Milei** has announced the country’s immediate withdrawal from the **World Health Organization (WHO)**. The decision follows in the footsteps of former U.S. President **Donald Trump**, who in 2020 also ordered the United States to leave the organization—though the move was later reversed by Joe Biden’s administration. Now, with Milei’s signature, Buenos Aires has officially severed ties with the WHO, marking a new chapter in the battle for national sovereignty against international bodies.

## **WHO Under Fire

The announcement was made by the Argentine government’s spokesperson, **Manuel Adorni**, in an explosive press conference:

*"President Milei has given clear instructions to Foreign Minister Gerardo Werthein for Argentina’s withdrawal from the WHO."*

And the justification for this decision is nothing short of damning:

*"The WHO promoted endless lockdowns without any scientific basis,"* declared the Argentine government. *"These policies kept children out of school, bankrupted businesses, pushed millions into unemployment, and cost us 130,000 lives."*

The official statement also accuses the WHO of imposing restrictive measures **without scientific backing**, leading to catastrophic consequences for the country’s economy and social fabric. According to Milei’s administration, **Argentina will no longer submit to decisions made by an organization it considers politicized and detached from reality**.

## **Milei vs. Globalist Health Policies

Argentina’s departure from the WHO reflects a growing criticism of international organizations that, according to their opponents, **have become instruments of power rather than institutions of global assistance**. In his speech, Adorni reinforced this view:

*"Argentines will not allow an international organization to interfere with our sovereignty, and certainly not with our health."*

The Argentine government argues that in recent years, the **WHO has demonstrated a complete inability to admit its mistakes and has instead continued to assume powers beyond its mandate**, directly undermining the sovereignty of its member states. For Milei, Argentina must regain **full control over its health policies**, free from the interference of international bureaucrats.

## **The Debate: WHO, Public Health, or Political Control?

Milei’s decision raises a crucial question that many governments have avoided addressing: **How far does the power of international organizations extend?** Does the WHO still fulfill its mission of promoting global well-being, or has it become a **political control tool** serving undisclosed agendas?

Critics of the WHO argue that the **COVID-19 pandemic was a turning point**, revealing how the organization can influence governments worldwide and impose measures that, according to them, are not necessarily based on science but rather on **political and economic interests**.

WHO supporters, on the other hand, claim that the organization is essential for global health coordination, arguing that **without it, the world would be adrift in times of crisis**. But is that really the case? That’s the question Milei, Trump, and millions of citizens worldwide have been asking.

## **International Reaction: Shock and Support

Argentina’s decision has sparked **strong reactions on the international stage**. While governments aligned with the global order express **concern**, conservative and nationalist movements view Milei as a **brave leader** standing up to the globalist establishment.

Behind the scenes, speculation is growing that other countries may follow suit, especially as the **WHO seeks to expand its powers and sign global treaties that could compel nations to adhere to centralized directives in future pandemics**.

Given this scenario, **Argentina’s exit from the WHO is not just symbolic**—it **could trigger a domino effect**, prompting other nations to question how much they should submit to decisions made by supranational entities.

## **Argentina’s Future Outside the WHO

Now, the question remains: what will Milei’s next steps be in shaping the country’s health policies? Argentina will pursue an **independent model**, establishing its own guidelines for future health crises. But one thing is certain: **Milei’s government has made it clear that it will not accept external impositions, reaffirming its commitment to national sovereignty**.

The real question that lingers is: **Will other leaders have the courage to follow this example?**
