## "Monkeys bite me! The world doesn’t spin, it flips!"

In a revelation that sounds more like a dystopian sci-fi plot than real life, Congresswoman Nancy Mace has exposed that over **$10 million** in taxpayer money was used to fund experiments creating **transgender mice, rats, and monkeys**. Yes, you read that right. While Americans struggle with inflation, skyrocketing housing costs, and economic uncertainty, the government has been busy funding bizarre and cruel animal experiments in the name of gender ideology.

During a congressional hearing, Mace detailed how these experiments included **surgically altering animals, administering hormone therapies, and studying transgender-related issues using taxpayer dollars**. The Biden administration, for instance, allocated **$2.5 million** to study the fertility of transgender mice. Another **$1 million** was spent investigating whether female rats injected with testosterone—simulating transgender men—were more likely to overdose on drugs commonly used in the LGBTQ community.

But the most disturbing revelation? Male monkeys were forcibly transitioned to female through hormone therapy to see if they became more susceptible to HIV—despite the fact that **monkeys cannot even contract human HIV**.

Nancy Mace did not hold back, condemning these experiments as a **waste of resources, unnecessary, and outright cruel**. She emphasized that **90% of animal testing fails when applied to humans**, making these studies not only ethically questionable but scientifically useless.

Justin Goodman, Senior Vice President of the White Coat Waste Project, testified that the U.S. government **spends over $20 billion annually** on animal experiments, many of which have no real-world application. He argued that modern alternatives, such as **advanced human-based models**, provide **far more accurate and ethical solutions**.

The burning question remains: **Why is the government so eager to push gender ideology, even at the expense of animals and taxpayers?** At a time when many Americans are questioning where their hard-earned money is going, these revelations add **one more reason to demand accountability and transparency** in public spending.

Nancy Mace vowed to keep fighting against this misuse of taxpayer money, hoping that exposing these practices will lead to **policy changes that protect both animals and American wallets**.
