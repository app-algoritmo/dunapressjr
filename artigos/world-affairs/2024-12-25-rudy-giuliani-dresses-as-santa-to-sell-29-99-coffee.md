Facing a $148 million defamation lawsuit, Rudy Giuliani has taken an unusual route to raise funds—dressing as Santa Claus to promote his $29.99 “Rudy Coffee.” In a quirky Christmas ad, Giuliani is seen cracking jokes while a woman sits on his lap, and elves hand out coffee like candy canes.

The ad, blending humor and self-awareness, has drawn mixed reactions online. Supporters praise Giuliani’s determination to address his financial troubles, while critics ridicule the unconventional marketing strategy. The ad's surreal tone has also made it a viral sensation, sparking memes and parodies.

Giuliani’s financial woes stem from his ongoing legal battles, including defamation claims related to the 2020 U.S. presidential election. The former New York City mayor has struggled to secure funds, reportedly auctioning memorabilia and launching various side ventures to stay afloat.

Despite the controversy, the coffee campaign has garnered significant attention, with initial reports suggesting a surge in sales. Whether this strategy will help Giuliani settle his debts remains uncertain, but it certainly reinforces his reputation as a polarizing and headline-grabbing figure.
