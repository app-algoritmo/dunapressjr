In recent years, many topics labeled as "conspiracy theories" by mainstream media and censored on social media have turned out to be true. From political scandals to digital censorship, public health issues, and geopolitics, the narrative imposed by the press and governments has proven to be far from infallible. Now, reality is emerging, proving that many so-called "conspiracy theorists" were actually right.

### **1. Hunter Biden’s Laptop

When the story of Hunter Biden's laptop surfaced shortly before the 2020 U.S. presidential election, mainstream media and social media platforms suppressed the information, claiming it was Russian disinformation. However, years later, even *The New York Times* and *The Washington Post* confirmed the authenticity of the files, which contained evidence of suspicious business dealings and corruption.

### **2. mRNA Vaccine Harm

During the pandemic, any mention of possible adverse effects of mRNA vaccines was quickly dismissed and even censored. However, recent studies and official pharmacovigilance data show a growing number of adverse reactions, including heart problems like myocarditis and pericarditis, as well as immune complications.

### **3. The "Plan-demic"

The idea that the pandemic was manipulated for political and economic purposes was dismissed as a conspiracy theory. However, revelations about the pharmaceutical industry's billion-dollar profits, conflicts of interest, and leaked internal documents show that the management of the health crisis was, at the very least, politicized and highly profitable for certain elites.

### **4. Lockdowns and Their Consequences

The unquestioned defense of lockdowns as the only solution to the pandemic ignored their devastating impacts. Today, it is proven that prolonged school closures harmed children's learning and mental health, while millions of small businesses collapsed, increasing inequalities and strengthening large corporations.

### **5. Ivermectin and Hydroxychloroquine

Two of the most controversial drugs in the fight against COVID-19 were labeled ineffective and dangerous. However, independent studies and recent scientific reviews suggest that these medications could have been useful in early treatment, especially when combined with other protocols.

### **6. Ineffectiveness of Face Masks

During the pandemic, any questioning of mask effectiveness was silenced. Today, studies show that cloth and surgical masks had limited impact in containing the virus, and some analyses indicate that prolonged use may have been more harmful than beneficial in certain contexts.

### **7. Social Media Censorship

For years, any opinion that diverged from the official narrative was banned or demonetized on platforms like Facebook, Twitter, and YouTube. In 2023, the release of the *Twitter Files* revealed how governments and tech companies collaborated to censor legitimate information under the guise of combating misinformation.

### **8. The Epstein Case and the Pedophilia Network

When Jeffrey Epstein was arrested, many speculated that he operated a global sex trafficking ring for powerful elites. After his controversial death in prison, evidence continued to emerge, confirming that he and his accomplice, Ghislaine Maxwell, were involved in a massive exploitation scheme that included high-profile figures.

### **9. Biolabs in Ukraine

The idea that secret biolabs funded by the U.S. existed in Ukraine was initially ridiculed by the media. However, documents from the U.S. government itself confirmed the existence of these laboratories, raising even more questions about their real purposes.

### **10. Child Trafficking

Reports about large-scale child trafficking were always dismissed as paranoia. However, law enforcement operations worldwide continue to dismantle international child exploitation networks, some of which are connected to powerful elites.

### **11. "RussiaGate" and the Steele Dossier

For years, the media pushed the narrative that Donald Trump and his campaign had conspired with Russia to manipulate the 2016 election. Later investigations revealed that *RussiaGate* was, in fact, a political setup orchestrated by the Democrats, and that the infamous Steele Dossier was filled with false information.

### **12. Corruption in Ukraine

Ukraine was portrayed as a model democracy under threat from Russia. However, recent investigations reveal high levels of corruption in the country, with billions of dollars sent as "aid" being misappropriated, along with suspicions of illicit business dealings involving Western politicians.

The pattern is clear: topics initially labeled as “conspiracy theories” frequently turn out to be true over time. Mainstream media and political establishments have a vested interest in controlling narratives, suppressing information that could undermine their credibility or expose hidden agendas. However, the truth always finds a way to surface.

The key lesson here is that we must question dominant narratives, seek independent sources, and not dismiss information simply because it is labeled as a “conspiracy theory.” After all, history has shown that so-called “conspiracy theorists” were often better informed than many mainstream journalists.
