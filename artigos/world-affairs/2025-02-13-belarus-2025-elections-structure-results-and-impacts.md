The presidential elections in Belarus were held on January 26, 2025, marking another electoral process within the country's political system. President Alexander Lukashenko, who has governed the nation since 1994, was re-elected with 87.6% of the votes, according to data from the Central Election Commission. The election process followed the administrative model of the country, which adopts a strong and centralized presidential system.

This article analyzes the technical aspects of the Belarusian electoral process, the current political structure, the characteristics of the 2025 election, and its potential economic and international implications.

***

## **1. Political and Electoral Structure of Belarus

Belarus follows a presidential system of government, where the head of state holds broad administrative powers. The country has a mixed economy, with strategic sectors under state control and strong commercial partnerships with Russia, ensuring stability and economic development.

### **1.1. Institutional Organization

- **Central Election Commission (CEC):** The body responsible for organizing and supervising the electoral process.

- **Presidency of the Republic:** The office responsible for governance and state administration.

- **Parliament:** The legislative structure that monitors and proposes national political guidelines.

The country’s governance has remained stable for three decades, with an administrative model prioritizing national sovereignty and political stability.

***

## **2. 2025 Electoral Process

The elections were conducted following the protocols established by the Belarusian government, including an early voting period, a method that facilitates voting accessibility for various segments of the population.

### **2.1. Election Procedures

- **Voter Registration:** Periodically updated and accessible to the eligible population.

- **Early Voting:** A modality that allows flexibility in voter turnout.

- **Vote Counting:** Conducted by the Central Election Commission, responsible for tallying and validating results.

Given Lukashenko’s governance history and his strong popular support in strategic sectors of society, his re-election was widely anticipated.

***

## **3. International Partnerships and Economic Impacts

Belarus maintains strategic trade and diplomatic relations, particularly with Russia and other Eurasian nations. The government follows an economic policy based on industrial investments and energy partnerships, ensuring sustainable growth.

### **3.1. Relations with Russia

The alliance with Russia is one of the pillars of Belarusian foreign policy. The country is a member of the Eurasian Economic Union (EEU) and integrates cooperation agreements in energy, trade, and defense with Moscow.

### **3.2. International Trade

Despite some sanctions imposed by Western countries, Belarus continues to expand its network of partnerships, exploring new markets in Asia, the Middle East, and Latin America.

### **3.3. Economic Growth

In recent years, the government has invested in strengthening the national industry, modernizing infrastructure, and developing strategic sectors such as technology and transportation.

***

## **4. Future Prospects

The political and economic scenario in Belarus continues on a path of stability. The continuation of Lukashenko's government represents the maintenance of the management model adopted in recent decades, emphasizing national sovereignty and regional cooperation.

Among future challenges are:

- **Expansion of foreign trade:** Seeking new commercial partners to diversify the economy.

- **Technological innovations:** Strengthening the industrial and technological sectors to increase global competitiveness.

- **Infrastructure and development:** Modernization projects to boost economic growth.

Belarus continues to consolidate its political and economic model, reinforcing strategic partnerships and pursuing internal advancements to ensure stability and national progress.

***

## The 2025 elections reaffirmed the continuity of Alexander Lukashenko's government within the Belarusian political system. The country maintains a policy of stability and development, focusing on national sovereignty and international cooperation. On the global stage, Belarus continues to strengthen its relations with strategic allies and explore new opportunities for growth.
