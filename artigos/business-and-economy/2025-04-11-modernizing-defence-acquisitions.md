## Modernizing Defense Acquisitions: 2025’s Most Transformative Move to Restore U.S. Supremacy

**Modernizing defense acquisitions** is no longer just a policy goal—it’s the driving force behind a bold new executive order that aims to reshape the future of America’s military, industrial, and economic strength.In a time of global instability, this landmark action signals the return of U.S. dominance through speed, innovation, and decisive reform. A new golden age begins with the transformation of how the United States equips and empowers its Armed Forces.

## The Return of American Power

*Modernizing Defense Acquisitions and Spurring Innovation in the Defense Industrial Base*, a new executive order issued directly from the White House, signals not just a policy shift—but a foundational transformation of the United States' strategic posture.

By addressing the inefficiencies that have long plagued the Pentagon’s procurement systems, this decisive act repositions the United States as the uncontested leader in industrial innovation, military strength, and economic growth.

At a time when global power dynamics are shifting, the White House’s directive aims to recapture what many consider the true essence of American exceptionalism: unmatched speed, scale, and strategic execution.

***

## Revolutionizing the Acquisition Process

The Executive Order mandates the Department of Defense to submit, within 60 days, a comprehensive plan to reform its acquisition systems. These changes will prioritize:

- **Commercial-first solutions** to promote cost-effective and innovative technologies.

- **Other Transactions Authority (OTA)** for faster contracting outside traditional rules.

- The adoption of **Rapid Capabilities Office** policies for agile execution.

The goal is clear: transform the acquisition cycle from a bureaucratic maze into a dynamic, tech-driven ecosystem that puts cutting-edge capabilities directly into the hands of the Armed Forces—fast.

***

## Empowering the Workforce for Innovation

A radical shift will also occur in how the acquisition workforce is structured and rewarded. No mais, aqueles que ousarem inovar e assumir riscos calculados serão os novos heróis da defesa nacional.

The new policy requires:

- Restructuring performance metrics for the acquisition workforce;

- Reorganizing the workforce to meet the demands of modern warfare;

- Deploying specialized field teams to train and accelerate projects in real time.

This professional revolution within the Department of Defense seeks to unleash the full potential of those on the “factory floor,” turning every desk, console, and laboratory into a strategic front.

***

## Cutting Bureaucracy and Accelerating Results

A key pillar of the Executive Order is its commitment to **deregulation and administrative simplification**.

The Secretary of Defense is ordered to apply the **ten-for-one rule**: for every new regulation, ten must be removed. This bold move aligns with the deregulatory push of Executive Order 14192 (*Unleashing Prosperity Through Deregulation*), targeting internal rules that hinder agility.

By slashing red tape, the United States regains its ability to act decisively—and build at the speed of conflict.

***

## Reviewing Major Defense Programs: Efficiency or Exit

Programs that are delayed, over budget, or underperforming will no longer be tolerated. Every **Major Defense Acquisition Program (MDAP)** must now prove its worth—or face cancellation.

Any program more than:

- **15% behind schedule**,

- **15% over budget**, or

- Failing to meet critical performance standards

...will be reviewed for termination. This ensures that only the most viable, mission-aligned projects will receive funding—clearing the way for superior alternatives.

***

## A Vision Rooted in Strength and Strategy

What sets this Executive Order apart is not just its ambition, but its clarity of purpose: **restore peace through strength**.

Rather than focusing on endless interventionism or reactive diplomacy, this policy invests in deterrence, self-sufficiency, and cutting-edge preparation. It harnesses the genius of America’s private sector while reinvigorating public defense infrastructure.

By aligning military needs with commercial innovation, the United States turns its economy into its strongest strategic asset.

***

## A Renewed Era of Prosperity and Peace

With this landmark order, the United States is not merely adjusting its defense strategy—it is declaring the beginning of a **new Golden Age**.

The modernization of acquisitions, streamlining of operations, and unleashing of workforce creativity will trigger a powerful chain reaction: revitalizing industry, securing peace through technological superiority, and restoring the United States’ global stature.

History may well mark this moment as the rebirth of an unshakable American supremacy.
