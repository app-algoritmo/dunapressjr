### **A Milestone for Economic Integration

The new free trade agreement between **EFTA** and Ukraine marks a significant step toward economic integration and post-war reconstruction. Signed in Kyiv on April 8, 2025, the deal between the four EFTA member states—Norway, Switzerland, Iceland, and Liechtenstein—and Ukraine introduces modern provisions to boost trade, investment, and cooperation in key areas such as digital commerce, sustainability, and market access.

Norwegian Minister of Trade and Industry, **Cecilie Myrseth**, described the deal as a cornerstone in Europe's collective efforts:

“This is an important milestone. The agreement significantly strengthens Ukraine’s political and economic integration with Europe and will drive development and economic growth.”

Her sentiments were echoed by **Yuliia Svyrydenko**, Ukraine’s First Deputy Prime Minister and Minister of Economy, who expressed her gratitude for EFTA’s continuous support. She emphasized that this modernized agreement offers mutual advantages, particularly in enhancing the standard of living and business opportunities.

### **New Opportunities for Trade and Investment

The updated agreement includes modern chapters designed to reflect today’s global economic challenges and innovations:

- **Digital Trade**: Facilitates secure and seamless cross-border electronic transactions.

- **Support for SMEs**: Helps small and medium-sized enterprises expand internationally.

- **Sustainable Development**: Encourages eco-friendly trade practices.

- **Agricultural Market Access**: Expands export potential for Ukrainian farmers.

- **Intellectual Property Rights**: Improves protections for innovation and brands.

- **Dispute Resolution**: Establishes a framework for fair and efficient handling of commercial conflicts.

Minister Myrseth emphasized:

“In these times, it is vital to promote rule-based open trade and strong business cooperation across Europe. This agreement creates new opportunities for companies in both Ukraine and Norway.”

### **Strengthening Economic Ties and Reconstruction

Beyond policy, the agreement carries a strong symbolic message of long-term collaboration. During her Kyiv visit, Myrseth engaged with business leaders from both nations to promote joint ventures and investment projects for Ukraine’s rebuilding process.

A significant highlight was the inauguration of the **Innovation Norway Kyiv office** and the **Norwegian-Ukrainian Business Hub**, established by the Norwegian-Ukrainian Chamber of Commerce to facilitate strategic economic partnerships.

“Norwegian businesses are eager to contribute to Ukraine’s reconstruction, now and post-war. This agreement sends a strong signal to invest in Ukraine and deepen our economic relationship,” Myrseth stated.

### **Bilateral Trade in Numbers

In 2024, trade between EFTA and Ukraine reached **€1.319 billion**, with notable growth in key sectors:

## Top EFTA Exports to Ukraine:

- Fish and seafood (€229M)

- Pharmaceuticals (€143M)

- Arms and ammunition (€77M)

- Vehicles (€77M)

- Electrical machinery (€44M)

## Top Imports from Ukraine to EFTA:

- Fats and oils (€37M)

- Precious stones and metals (€26M)

- Textiles and garments (€22M)

- Furniture and mattresses (€20M)

- Beverages and spirits (€20M)

This exchange reflects not only a strong commercial relationship but also mutual dependencies in strategic industries.

### **Long-Term Strategic Impact

This modernized agreement positions Ukraine as a more integrated player in the European market while reinforcing EFTA’s commitment to democratic values, open economies, and regional stability. As Ukraine works toward European Union membership, the EFTA agreement sets a precedent for deeper cooperation and regulatory alignment.

It also enhances investor confidence by offering transparent legal frameworks and dispute resolution mechanisms—critical factors for long-term investment and recovery efforts.

The updated free trade agreement between EFTA and Ukraine is more than a commercial tool—it is a diplomatic gesture, a strategic framework, and a developmental roadmap. It opens new paths for economic cooperation, resilience, and integration.

As Ukraine rebuilds, this partnership ensures that prosperity and peace remain within reach for all involved parties.

The revised EFTA-Ukraine free trade agreement represents a significant stride towards bolstering economic ties and fostering mutual prosperity. By facilitating increased integration into the European market, Ukraine's trajectory towards EU membership is further solidified. Moreover, the agreement serves as a testament to the shared values of democratic principles, economic openness, and regional stability upheld by EFTA and Ukraine.
