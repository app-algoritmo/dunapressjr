The importance of a system designed to provide social well-being from an early age lies in fostering an equitable and resilient society. When young people have access to programs that promote financial security, quality education, and housing opportunities, they can build a strong foundation for their future. These systems not only ensure that basic needs are met but also empower individuals to achieve autonomy and prosperity, reducing social inequalities. In countries like Norway, policies such as BSU and Husbanken exemplify how well-planned strategies can turn challenges into opportunities, creating a continuous cycle of growth and stability for future generations.

In Norway, young people have access to innovative programs that make homeownership more achievable. Two of the most impactful initiatives are **BSU (Boligsparing for Ungdom)** and **Husbanken**.

## **BSU (Boligsparing for Ungdom)

BSU is a home savings scheme designed for individuals under the age of 34 who aim to save for their first home. Key benefits include:

1. **Tax Deductions:** Save up to NOK 27,500 annually and deduct 10% of this from your income tax, with a maximum annual deduction of NOK 2,750. (DNB Bank)

2. **High-Interest Rates:** BSU accounts typically offer better interest rates compared to regular savings accounts. (Lifein Norway)

3. **Savings Limit:** You can save up to NOK 300,000 over time. (Norwegian Tax Authority)

Funds must be used for purchasing or maintaining a primary residence. As of 2021, they can also finance renovations.

***

## **Husbanken (Norwegian State Housing Bank)

Husbanken is Norway’s governmental agency supporting affordable housing initiatives. Its key services include:

1. **Housing Grants:** Assists individuals with low income and high housing costs. (Husbanken Official)

2. **Start Loans:** Offered through municipalities to help people buy or retain their homes. (Husbanken Start Loans)

3. **Construction and Renovation Loans:** Directly supports building or upgrading homes, with a focus on environmentally sustainable designs. (About Husbanken)

Husbanken ensures that housing is accessible to vulnerable groups while promoting sustainability and inclusivity.

Both BSU and Husbanken play vital roles in Norway’s housing policies, empowering young people to navigate the property market effectively while benefiting from financial incentives and support systems.
