In a bold and symbolic move, President Donald Trump announced on Friday (9) that he will revoke former President Joe Biden’s security clearance, preventing him from continuing to receive the traditional daily intelligence briefings.

**"There is no need for Joe Biden to continue receiving access to classified information. Therefore, we are immediately revoking his security clearance and stopping his daily intelligence briefings,"** Trump declared in a Truth Social post.

This decision marks a historic reversal and follows the precedent set by Biden himself in 2021 when he ordered the U.S. Intelligence Community to block Trump’s access to national security information—a courtesy typically granted to former presidents.

## **The Precedent and Trump’s Justification

Trump made it clear that Biden was the first to cut off his predecessor’s access, refusing to provide intelligence briefings to the 45th President of the United States. Now, the tables have turned.

The current president also cited the **Hur Report**, which revealed that Biden suffers from **“poor memory”** and that even in his prime, he was not considered trustworthy enough to handle sensitive information.

**"The Hur Report revealed that Biden suffers from ‘poor memory’ and, even in his ‘prime,’ could not be trusted with sensitive information,"** Trump wrote.

And, as expected, he concluded his post with his signature style:

**"I will always protect our National Security — JOE, YOU’RE FIRED! MAKE AMERICA GREAT AGAIN!"**

## **A New Era in the White House

Trump’s decision reinforces his commitment to national security and highlights the changes he plans to implement in his new term. His return to the White House is already marked by strong measures to reverse Biden’s policies and reassert his leadership.

By revoking Biden’s access, Trump is not only protecting state secrets but also sending a clear message: the era of concessions and privileges for incompetent politicians is over.

Now, the question remains—how will the Democrats and Biden react? It looks like the former president will have to keep up with U.S. affairs just like any other citizen: by watching the news on TV.
