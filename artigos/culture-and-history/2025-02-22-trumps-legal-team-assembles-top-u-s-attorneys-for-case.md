## **Trump’s Lawyers Suing Alexandre Are Legal Heavyweights

The legal battle between Donald Trump and Minister Alexandre de Moraes takes on new dimensions with the revelation of the lawyers representing the former U.S. president. **Matthew Schwartz** and **Martin De Luca**, both former prosecutors from the powerful **Southern District of New York**, have taken the lead in the lawsuit, bringing with them impeccable legal backgrounds and international recognition. The appointment of two of the most renowned attorneys in the U.S. legal system underscores the seriousness of the case against Moraes.

## **Who Are Trump’s Lawyers?

The names chosen to lead Trump’s legal offensive are not ordinary figures in the legal world. Both built outstanding careers in one of the most feared and respected jurisdictions in the United States, specializing in financial crimes, public corruption, and terrorism.

### **Matthew Schwartz: The "Methodical Assassin"

Currently the president of the prestigious law firm **Boies Schiller Flexner**, **Matthew Schwartz** served as a federal prosecutor in Manhattan for ten years (2005–2015), leading the **Securities and Commodities Fraud Task Force**. During this period, he was at the forefront of some of the most impactful investigations in the U.S., including:

- **Bernie Madoff’s Ponzi scheme** – one of the biggest financial scandals in history;

- **The JPMorgan "London Whale" case** – a multi-billion-dollar loss for the bank due to high-risk market bets.

Schwartz has been described by *Forbes* as a **“methodical assassin in the courtroom”**, known for his **surgical precision and focus**. His excellence has been recognized with **three John Marshall Awards**, the highest honor given to lawyers by the U.S. Department of Justice.

### **Martin De Luca: The International Case Expert

Like Schwartz, **Martin De Luca** also built a solid reputation as a **Special Assistant Prosecutor** in the **Southern District of New York**. In recent days, his name has returned to the spotlight after successfully **negotiating the release of Marc Fogel**, an American professor imprisoned in Russia for carrying medical cannabis. His decisive role in this case demonstrates his **expertise in sensitive diplomatic cases and international negotiations**.

De Luca has been praised by **Chambers & Partners** as an **"excellent and highly sophisticated lawyer."** His experience includes **public corruption investigations, financial fraud, and terrorism cases**, working closely with the **FBI and U.S. intelligence agencies**.

## **Trump’s Choice: A Clear Message

Selecting attorneys of this caliber for the case against Alexandre de Moraes signals that Trump does not see this dispute as merely political but rather as a **high-stakes legal battle**. The fact that both lawyers have experience handling major financial investigations and international cases may indicate the strategy Trump intends to pursue.

The **Southern District of New York**, where Schwartz and De Luca worked, is known for handling high-profile cases against **Wall Street tycoons, influential political figures, and criminal organizations**. If there is any place in the U.S. where justice does not hesitate to take on powerful names, it is in this jurisdiction. This reinforces the idea that Trump is playing a high-stakes game and bringing in the best to fight his battle.

## **Impact on the International Scene

The involvement of Schwartz and De Luca in this case could elevate its international relevance. Both lawyers have connections to **top levels of the U.S. government, the FBI, and intelligence agencies**, which could make this dispute even more significant diplomatically. Moreover, the U.S. media has already begun to highlight their role, potentially increasing pressure on the case.

By assembling a legal team of this caliber, Trump is demonstrating that he is willing to take his fight against Moraes to the **highest legal levels**. Schwartz and De Luca bring with them a **track record of victories in extremely complex cases**, suggesting that this legal battle will be intense and widely scrutinized. Now, all eyes are on the next steps of a lawsuit that is set to unfold in the most powerful courts in the world.
