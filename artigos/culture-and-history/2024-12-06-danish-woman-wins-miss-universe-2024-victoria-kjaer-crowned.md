The Danish beauty **Victoria Kjaer**, aged 21, was crowned **Miss Universe 2024** on Saturday night, December 16, during the 73rd edition of the iconic competition held in Mexico City. In a glamorous and emotional ceremony, Victoria received the crown in the early hours of Sunday, succeeding **Sheynnis Palacios** of Nicaragua, Miss Universe 2023.

Victoria stood out among contestants from over 80 countries, impressing the judges with her beauty, charisma, and impactful responses during the interview rounds. A student of International Relations, the young Dane also gained attention for her passionate advocacy for environmental sustainability and women's rights—key themes of her social project presented during the competition.

## **A Historic Moment for Denmark

Victoria’s victory marks a significant moment in Denmark's history at Miss Universe, as the country had not won the title for decades. Her elegant and authentic performance moved not only her fellow Danes but also viewers worldwide who followed the event.

"I am grateful to my family, my country, and everyone who believed in me. I want to use this platform to inspire women to dream big and contribute to a better world," Victoria said during her coronation speech.

## **The Ceremony Highlights

The night was filled with vibrant cultural performances celebrating Mexico's rich heritage, featuring stunning musical acts and choreography. The event also included a panel of esteemed judges, featuring fashion industry leaders, former Miss Universe winners, and international celebrities.

Victoria faced strong competition from finalists representing countries such as India, Colombia, the Philippines, and Brazil, all delivering powerful speeches and presenting impactful projects. However, it was her authenticity and eloquence that captured the hearts of the judges and the audience.

## **What’s Next for Victoria Kjaer

As Miss Universe, Victoria will embark on a global journey, championing social causes, supporting humanitarian initiatives, and participating in sustainability campaigns. Over the coming months, her mission will be to inspire and engage communities worldwide, reaffirming the competition’s role as a platform for empowerment and positive change.

Victoria Kjaer's victory is a celebration of diversity and female empowerment, proving that Miss Universe continues to be a stage for inspiring voices aiming to transform the world.
