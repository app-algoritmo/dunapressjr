In a surprising and symbolic move, Donald Trump, the newly elected President of the United States, announced the appointment of actors Mel Gibson, Sylvester Stallone, and Jon Voight as “special ambassadors” to Hollywood. The initiative has been widely publicized as a way to revitalize the American film industry and address the recent challenges faced by the sector.

## An Industry in Transition

The American entertainment industry has faced tough times in recent years, including disruptions caused by the COVID-19 pandemic, writer and actor strikes, and an overall decline in production. Since 2021, film and television production has dropped by about 26%, according to industry reports. With this appointment, Trump aims to highlight the need to revitalize the sector and attract new investments to the market.

## The Appointees

The three actors chosen for this role are not only cinema legends but also have histories of supporting Trump. Mel Gibson, known for his blockbuster hits and controversies throughout his career, expressed surprise at the invitation but stated he was willing to collaborate. Sylvester Stallone, star of “Rocky” and “Rambo,” is a frequent visitor to Mar-a-Lago and has compared Trump to George Washington. Jon Voight, an Academy Award winner and one of Trump’s most vocal supporters in Hollywood, also accepted the role enthusiastically.

## A Symbolic Blow

Although the term “special ambassador” does not correspond to an official diplomatic position, the appointment carries significant symbolic weight. Trump indicated that the actors’ role will be to represent the interests of the industry and work to restore Hollywood’s influence on the global stage.

Additionally, the choice of such prominent and polarizing figures is seen as a direct message against what Trump has called “corrupt practices” in Hollywood. Some of his supporters have interpreted the initiative as a “massive blow” to alleged “pedophiles” and obscure networks of influence within the film industry.

## Mixed Reactions

The appointment has elicited varied reactions. While Trump’s supporters praised the selection as a way to reaffirm traditional values and clean up Hollywood’s reputation, critics argued that the measure is purely symbolic and impractical.

So far, no specific details have been disclosed about the role the three actors will play or the initiatives they will promote under their leadership.

## The Future of Hollywood

Regardless of the criticism, the appointment of Mel Gibson, Sylvester Stallone, and Jon Voight reflects Trump’s intention to reinforce his cultural and political impact on one of the United States’ most iconic sectors. Time will tell whether this initiative will bring the expected results or remain a symbolic gesture in the country’s turbulent political and cultural landscape.
