The pursuit of financial independence is a fundamental pillar of women's empowerment, enabling them to have the freedom to make decisions about their personal and professional lives. In an increasingly dynamic world, building a solid and well-planned financial foundation from an early age is essential for women to achieve their goals without having to sacrifice their dreams of starting a family, becoming matriarchs, or developing a successful career.

## The Example of Norway and the Value of Early Work

Countries like Norway demonstrate the positive impact of encouraging young people to enter the workforce early, often at the age of 16, typically working 20 hours per week in paid employment. This is a prerequisite for earning the points needed to access university education. This early experience develops fundamental skills such as time management, responsibility, and financial planning. These competencies help women not only adapt to the job market but also excel in it.

Following this model, young people can avoid the "capitalism loop"—impulsive and unplanned consumption—and focus on accumulating experiences and resources to achieve the much-desired financial independence. Prioritizing education and work from an early age allows women to build a stable financial foundation by the age of 40, opening doors to pursue personal and professional projects with freedom and confidence.

In this context, the role of parents is crucial in encouraging young people, especially girls, to start their professional journeys early, equipping them to become independent and promising adults. The role of parents goes beyond being mere friends or providers of their children's desires; their true mission is to educate and guide their children, fostering personal development and preparing them for prosperous and fulfilling adult lives. Even when it requires facing emotional blackmail from their children, it is essential for parents to stay committed to shaping responsible and resilient individuals.

## Education and Planning: The Keys to Success

Women's empowerment is rooted in education and financial planning. Investing savings in education and professional development is a secure path to building a solid and successful trajectory. Women who avoid trivialities and prioritize their education not only secure a promising future but also gain the ability to make more conscious and strategic decisions. This approach allows them to choose the ideal time to become mothers or matriarchs, avoiding the burden of excessive roles and dedicating themselves fully to each phase of their lives with balance and purpose.

Education should be seen as a powerful tool for transforming realities. The sooner women understand the importance of planning their financial and professional lives, the sooner they can prepare to balance motherhood with a successful career. With organization and clear goals, they can build a robust financial foundation, ensuring security and freedom to decide the best time to start a family or expand their professional horizons.

## Balancing Motherhood and Career

Financial planning and career dedication do not mean giving up the dream of motherhood or becoming a matriarch. On the contrary, they enable women to choose the right moment to embrace this experience without compromising their professional achievements. It is possible to build a balanced life where personal and family fulfillment coexist harmoniously.

Maye Musk, mother of visionary Elon Musk, shares an inspiring journey of resilience and planning in her book. As a model, nutritionist, and mother of three successful children, Maye reveals how strategic planning was essential to balancing her children's upbringing with the advancement of her own career. In her narrative, she highlights the importance of cultivating resilience, setting clear goals, and teaching her children to dream big, while demonstrating that even in the face of financial and personal challenges, it is possible to build a prosperous future with determination and well-thought-out strategies.

Prof. Dr. Natália Bellan is an inspiring example of how it is possible to build an outstanding international career without giving up her role as a mother and wife. With a trajectory marked by academic excellence, she has gained recognition in the field of medical device regulation and advanced technologies, successfully balancing her professional responsibilities with her dedication to family. Natália demonstrates that with planning, determination, and mutual support, it is possible to balance a successful career with a fulfilling family life, proving that women can conquer the world without compromising their values and personal dreams.

## O Exemplo da Noruega e o Valor do Trabalho Precoce

Países como a Noruega demonstram o impacto positivo de incentivar jovens a entrarem no mercado de trabalho desde cedo, muitas vezes a partir dos 16 anos, normalmente 20 horas semanais remunerado. Uma exigência para conseguirem pontuações necessárias para estudarem na universidade. Essa experiência inicial desenvolve habilidades fundamentais, como gestão do tempo, responsabilidade e planejamento financeiro. Essas competências ajudam mulheres a não apenas se adaptarem ao mercado, mas também a se destacarem nele.

Seguindo este modelo, jovens podem evitar o "looping do capitalismo" — o consumo impulsivo e sem planejamento — e focar em acumular experiências e recursos para alcançar a tão desejada independência. Priorizar educação e trabalho desde cedo permite que, até os 40 anos, as mulheres possam ter uma base financeira estável, abrindo portas para realizar seus projetos pessoais e profissionais com liberdade e confiança.

Nesse contexto, o papel dos pais é fundamental ao incentivar jovens, especialmente meninas, a iniciarem sua jornada profissional desde cedo, capacitando-os para se tornarem adultos independentes e promissores. O papel dos pais vai além de ser apenas amigos ou provedores de desejos; sua verdadeira missão é educar e orientar os filhos, promovendo seu desenvolvimento pessoal e preparando-os para uma vida adulta próspera e realizada. Mesmo que, muitas vezes, isso exija enfrentar chantagens emocionais vindas dos próprios filhos, é essencial que os pais mantenham seu compromisso com a formação de indivíduos responsáveis e resilientes.

O fortalecimento feminino tem suas bases na educação e no planejamento financeiro. Investir economias em estudos e no desenvolvimento profissional é um caminho seguro para construir uma trajetória sólida e bem-sucedida. Mulheres que evitam futilidades e priorizam sua formação garantem não apenas um futuro promissor, mas também a capacidade de tomar decisões mais conscientes e estratégicas. Essa abordagem permite que escolham o momento ideal para serem mães e matriarcas, evitando a sobrecarga de funções e se dedicando plenamente a cada fase de suas vidas, com equilíbrio e propósito.

A educação deve ser vista como uma ferramenta poderosa para transformar realidades. É possível construir uma vida equilibrada, onde a realização pessoal e a familiar caminhem lado a lado.

Natália demonstra que, com planejamento, determinação e apoio mútuo, é possível equilibrar uma carreira de sucesso com uma vida familiar plena, provando que mulheres podem conquistar o mundo sem deixar de lado seus valores e sonhos pessoais.
