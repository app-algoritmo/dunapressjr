## **IMPOSING SANCTIONS ON THE INTERNATIONAL CRIMINAL COURT

### Contextualizing the Sanctions Against the ICC

On February 2025, President Donald J. Trump issued an executive order imposing sanctions on the International Criminal Court (ICC). This decision stems from the U.S. government's firm stance that the ICC has no jurisdiction over the United States or its allies, particularly Israel. The executive order was justified under various legal frameworks, including the International Emergency Economic Powers Act (IEEPA), the National Emergencies Act (NEA), and the Immigration and Nationality Act, among others.

The Trump administration has consistently viewed the ICC as an overreaching institution that unfairly targets the United States and its allies. In this particular instance, the ICC's investigations and issuance of arrest warrants against Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu and former Minister of Defense Yoav Gallant were cited as overreaches of jurisdiction. Given that neither the United States nor Israel are signatories to the Rome Statute, the legal foundation of the ICC, the executive order asserts that the court lacks legitimacy in its actions against these nations.

### The Justification for the Sanctions

The executive order presents several key arguments in favor of the sanctions:

1. **Lack of Jurisdiction** – The ICC's assertion of jurisdiction over non-member states is deemed illegitimate, given that both the United States and Israel have never ratified the Rome Statute.

2. **Threat to National Security** – The actions of the ICC are described as a direct threat to U.S. sovereignty, as they expose American military personnel and government officials to potential prosecution by an international body that lacks their consent.

3. **Protection of Allied Nations** – The sanctions also serve as a protective measure for Israel, which has been targeted by ICC investigations despite its democratic governance and adherence to international laws of warfare.

4. **Violation of Sovereignty** – The U.S. maintains that subjecting American citizens and allies to the ICC’s jurisdiction without their consent infringes upon their sovereign rights and undermines international diplomatic norms.

### Specific Measures Imposed by the Executive Order

To counter the ICC’s actions, the executive order outlines several punitive measures:

- **Blocking Property and Financial Assets** – All property and financial interests of designated ICC officials, employees, and affiliates that fall under U.S. jurisdiction are frozen and cannot be accessed or transferred.

- **Travel and Entry Restrictions** – ICC personnel, along with their immediate family members, are barred from entering the United States.

- **Ban on Financial Contributions** – Donations or financial support to individuals sanctioned under this order are strictly prohibited.

- **Criminalization of Evasion** – Any attempt to circumvent these sanctions, including indirect financial assistance or resource allocation, is subject to legal penalties.

- **Report on Additional Persons** – Within 60 days, the Secretary of the Treasury must submit a report identifying additional individuals who may be subject to these sanctions.

### Implications for International Relations

The sanctions against the ICC mark a significant escalation in the longstanding tension between the United States and the international judicial body. While the U.S. has historically opposed the ICC’s jurisdiction over its citizens, this executive order represents one of the strongest rebukes to the court's authority.

### **For the United States:

The executive order reinforces American sovereignty and sends a clear message that any attempt to prosecute U.S. citizens or military personnel without consent will be met with tangible consequences. It also aligns with previous legislative measures, such as the American Servicemembers’ Protection Act of 2002, which was designed to shield U.S. military personnel from ICC prosecution.

### **For Israel:

By explicitly including Israel in the sanctions framework, the U.S. reaffirms its unwavering support for the nation amidst rising legal and political challenges on the international stage. The decision could further strengthen diplomatic and military cooperation between the two allies.

### **For the ICC:

These sanctions may place significant pressure on the ICC and its officials, particularly in terms of financial constraints and global legitimacy. The move could also lead to broader international debate over the limits of ICC jurisdiction and the balance between international accountability and national sovereignty.

President Trump's executive order imposing sanctions on the ICC is a decisive measure aimed at defending U.S. and allied interests against what it perceives as unlawful overreach by an international tribunal. The order underscores the U.S. commitment to protecting its military personnel, government officials, and allies from unwarranted legal actions by a court that lacks jurisdiction over them. While this move is likely to provoke criticism from international legal bodies and human rights organizations, it signals a strong and unwavering stance on national sovereignty and geopolitical strategy.
