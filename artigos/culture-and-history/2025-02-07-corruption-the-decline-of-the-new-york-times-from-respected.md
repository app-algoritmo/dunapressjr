The *New York Times* was once one of the most respected newspapers in the world. Founded in 1851, it became a reference in investigative journalism, exposing political scandals, uncovering international conflicts, and revealing truths that helped shape American and global society. However, over the past few decades, the newspaper has undergone a rapid ethical and moral decline, turning into a propaganda vehicle serving obscure interests. Today, the *New York Times* is nothing more than a cog in the machine of information manipulation, corrupted by public and private money, and serving agendas that have little to do with truth and justice.

## **The Fall of a Giant

The decline of the *New York Times* can be traced to the moment it abandoned its commitment to impartial investigative journalism and began acting as a political agent. Instead of questioning power and defending transparency, the newspaper became a tool for manipulation, distorting facts to benefit certain groups and harm others.

One of the key factors in this transformation was the influence of entities like the *U.S. Agency for International Development (USAID)*, a branch of the U.S. government frequently involved in influence operations and propaganda. Supposedly dedicated to global development, USAID has been exposed for funding media projects aligned with specific political interests, often undermining national sovereignty and manipulating narratives to justify foreign interventions.

By accepting indirect funding and aligning its editorial stance with the interests of these institutions, the *New York Times* ceased to be a trustworthy newspaper and became an instrument of disinformation. It now publishes articles that follow a pattern of reality distortion, pushing political agendas, censoring inconvenient information, and launching attacks against anyone who dares to challenge its dogmas.

## **Deceptive Narratives and the Fabrication of Truth

The corruption of the *New York Times* is evident not only in the topics it chooses to cover but also in how it reports them. Serious investigative journalism has been replaced by an information warfare strategy, where half-truths and distortions are used to construct favorable narratives.

Cases such as the selective coverage of elections worldwide, the manipulation of information about international conflicts, and the relentless attacks on political opponents of certain groups demonstrate its lack of commitment to truth. The newspaper, which should serve as a guardian of democracy and press freedom, has become a vehicle for coordinated disinformation, promoting censorship disguised as "fact-checking" and targeted attacks against those who question its established narratives.

## **The Role of Billionaires and Globalists in Corrupting Journalism

Beyond public money funneled through organizations like USAID, the *New York Times* has also surrendered to the interests of billionaires and globalist groups seeking to control the flow of information. Powerful figures in the financial world, major tech corporations, and globalist entities identified the newspaper as a useful platform to shape public opinion and advance their own agendas.

Funding from foundations and investors aligned with specific political and social projects has directly influenced the content published by the newspaper. This means that instead of adhering to journalistic principles of neutrality and independence, the *New York Times* now produces content tailored to please its sponsors, compromising any remaining shred of credibility.

## **The Domino Effect: How the Corruption of the *New York Times* Affects Global Journalism

The corruption of the *New York Times* does not only affect the United States; it reverberates globally. As one of the most influential newspapers in the world, its narratives are replicated by other media outlets that blindly trust its publications. This creates a domino effect where lies and distortions are amplified and spread on an international scale.

The newspaper's influence on global journalism means that entire countries are affected by its manipulation strategies. Governments are either toppled or strengthened based on orchestrated media campaigns. Politicians rise to power or are destroyed depending on the interests of their financial backers. Social movements are either inflated or ignored depending on what best serves the agenda in play. And all of this happens under the facade of respectable journalism.

## **The Death of Journalism and the Awakening of Truth

The *New York Times* is no longer a newspaper—it is a tool of manipulation, an instrument for creating narratives that benefit groups seeking to exert control over information. What was once a beacon of press freedom has become a pillar of censorship and disinformation.

However, despite media corruption, the truth cannot be silenced forever. The public is waking up to the reality that traditional newspapers, once reliable, are now propaganda machines. The rise of independent media and the search for alternative sources of information show that media manipulation has an expiration date.

What happened to the *New York Times* is a reflection of the decline of global journalism. But as long as there are those willing to question, investigate, and expose the truth, there is hope that the press will one day reclaim its fundamental role: informing with integrity, without serving shady interests.

This article is just the first in a series that will reveal how corruption has taken over global media. Other newspapers and media outlets will also be exposed because the truth must be told—and the real press, which still resists, must be strengthened.
