## 5 Playlists for Sleepless Nights in 2025 | J&M Duna Press

### The Magic of a Midnight Playlist

It’s 4:10 a.m. on a quiet Sunday, October 5, 2025. The world outside is still, save for the occasional hum of a distant car or the soft rustle of leaves in the autumn breeze. You’re wide awake, staring at the ceiling, your mind racing through thoughts that refuse to settle. Maybe it’s the weight of the week ahead, the echo of a conversation, or just the strange, restless energy that comes with the witching hour. Whatever it is, sleep isn’t coming—but music might.

Music has a unique power to anchor us when the world feels adrift. It’s a companion for the sleepless, a guide through the labyrinth of late-night thoughts. At Duna Press, we believe in the transformative power of stories—whether told through words, images, or sound. So, inspired by those restless moments, we’ve curated five playlists to carry you through the sleepless nights of 2025. Each one is designed to meet you where you are, whether you’re seeking calm, nostalgia, or a journey to another world. Let’s dive in.

### Playlist 1: Tokyo Lo-Fi Dreams

## Vibe: A neon-lit stroll through an empty city

!Five Playlists to Conquer Sleepless Nights in 2025

Imagine walking through Shibuya at midnight, the neon signs flickering above empty streets, a soft rain tapping on your umbrella. That’s the essence of *Tokyo Lo-Fi Dreams*. Anchored by the legendary Nujabes, this playlist blends lo-fi hip-hop with jazzy undertones, creating a soundscape that’s both meditative and cinematic. Tracks like “Ain’t No Mystery” and “Feather” feel like a warm hug from a city that never sleeps but knows how to dream.

Why it works: Lo-fi’s repetitive beats and soft melodies tap into the brain’s need for rhythm and calm, as studies from the University of Cambridge (2023) suggest. It’s perfect for journaling, reflecting, or just letting your thoughts drift. Pair it with a cup of chamomile tea, and you’re halfway to serenity.

## Key Tracks:

- Nujabes – “Feather (feat. Cise Starr & Akin)”

- Joji – “Slow Dancing in the Dark (Lo-Fi Remix)”

- Chillhop Essentials – “Rainy Days”

**Where to Find It:** [Spotify – Tokyo Lo-Fi Dreams](https://open.spotify.com/playlist/37i9dQZF1DX9qye2Z8TK7a)

### Playlist 2: Bedroom Indie Blues

## Vibe: Heartache with a side of hope

!Five Playlists to Conquer Sleepless Nights in 2025

For those nights when your heart feels heavy but you’re not ready to give up, *Bedroom Indie Blues* is your go-to. This playlist channels the raw emotion of indie rock, with artists like Phoebe Bridgers and Big Thief leading the charge. Start with “Motion Sickness” for its aching honesty, then let Big Thief’s “Not” carry you into a cathartic release. It’s music for crying, dancing, or just staring out the window at the stars.

Why it works: Indie music’s storytelling resonates with the human need for connection, as noted in a 2024 study from the Journal of Music Psychology. It’s like having a friend who gets it, no questions asked.

## Key Tracks:

- Phoebe Bridgers – “Motion Sickness”

- Big Thief – “Not”

- Julien Baker – “Appointments”

**Where to Find It:** [Spotify – Bedroom Indie Blues](https://open.spotify.com/playlist/37i9dQZF1DX2UgsUI2EL6u)

### Playlist 3: Bossa Nova ao Vivo

## Vibe: A smoky café in Rio, 1960s style

!Five Playlists to Conquer Sleepless Nights in 2025

There’s something timeless about bossa nova—it’s the sound of a simpler era, yet it feels alive and urgent. *Bossa Nova ao Vivo* is all about raw, unpolished performances from legends like Tom Jobim and João Gilberto. Think “Garota de Ipanema” recorded live, with every crackle of the mic and murmur of the crowd intact. It’s music that reminds you to breathe, to feel the pulse of life.

Why it works: Bossa nova’s syncopated rhythms and gentle melodies lower cortisol levels, according to a 2023 study in *Frontiers in Psychology*. It’s perfect for unwinding or even slow-dancing alone in your living room.

## Key Tracks:

- João Gilberto – “Chega de Saudade (Live)”

- Tom Jobim – “Águas de Março”

- Elis Regina – “O Barquinho”

**Where to Find It:** [Spotify – Bossa Nova ao Vivo](https://open.spotify.com/playlist/37i9dQZF1DX0A8zVlWWT5y)

### Playlist 4: Space Ambient Odyssey

## Vibe: Floating through a cosmic void

If you’ve ever wanted to feel like an astronaut drifting above Earth, *Space Ambient Odyssey* is your ticket. This playlist, featuring artists like Tycho and Bonobo, weaves minimalist electronica with ambient textures. Tracks like Tycho’s “A Walk” or Bonobo’s “Cirrus” create a sense of weightlessness, perfect for those nights when you want to escape your own head.

Why it works: Ambient music engages the brain’s default mode network, promoting introspection and calm, as per a 2025 meta-analysis in *Neuroscience Letters*. It’s ideal for meditation or visualizing your place in the universe.

## Key Tracks:

- Tycho – “A Walk”

- Bonobo – “Cirrus”

- Jon Hopkins – “Open Eye Signal”

**Where to Find It:** [Spotify – Space Ambient Odyssey](https://open.spotify.com/playlist/37i9dQZF1DX1s9knj1sznd)

### Playlist 5: Chansons Rétro

## Vibe: A Parisian café with a modern twist

Close your eyes and picture a dimly lit café in Paris, 1967. Now add a modern reverb and a touch of electronic flair. *Chansons Rétro* brings together the sultry voices of Dalida and Françoise Hardy with remixes that feel fresh in 2025. Tracks like Hardy’s “Tous les garçons et les filles” or Dalida’s “Bambino” get a subtle glow-up, blending nostalgia with a contemporary edge.

Why it works: Nostalgic music triggers dopamine release, per a 2024 study in *Nature Human Behaviour*, making it a perfect mood-lifter for late-night ruminations.

## Key Tracks:

- Françoise Hardy – “Tous les garçons et les filles (Remix)”

- Dalida – “Bambino (Modern Edit)”

- Edith Piaf – “Non, je ne regrette rien”

**Where to Find It:** [Spotify – Chansons Rétro](https://open.spotify.com/playlist/37i9dQZF1DX2sQz4T7uF6y)
