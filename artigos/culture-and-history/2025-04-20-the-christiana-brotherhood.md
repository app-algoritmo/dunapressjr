## The Christiana Brotherhood is a spiritual alliance founded in 2025 to proclaim truth, resist the antichrist spirit, and strengthen believers in Christ’s sovereignty.

In a world that grows darker with each passing day, many still search for a place where light is not just spoken of, but lived. The **Christiana Brotherhood** was born from that need — the longing to remain firm when the world bends, to hold the Cross high when others drop their weapons, and to stand for Christ when truth becomes unpopular. This is not a movement of trends, but a gathering of the faithful — those who know that silence is no longer an option.

Founded in 2025, The Christiana Brotherhood rises as a spiritual response to the moral and ideological degradation of the modern world, gathering faithful remnants under the sovereignty of Christ.

## **The Birth of the Christiana Brotherhood

In a time marked by spiritual confusion, moral collapse, and ideological manipulation, the **Christiana Brotherhood** emerges as a bold and consecrated response. Founded in 2025, it is more than an institution — it is a spiritual call to the faithful.

This international alliance gathers men and women who recognize Christ as the **Alpha and Omega**, the One who reigns over time, nations, and eternity. The Brotherhood seeks to unite those who still stand, unwavering, in the midst of a world that increasingly rejects truth, light, and holiness.

## **Symbols and Spiritual Identity

The symbol of the Christiana Brotherhood is a crowned cross, flanked by the letters **Alpha (Α)** and **Omega (Ω)** — representing Christ, the beginning and the end. This emblem is not merely aesthetic. It is a declaration of allegiance and resistance.

The Brotherhood is **not a church or denomination**, but a spiritual order. It does not compete with congregations — it collaborates in silence, discernment, and strength.

*“We are not an institution of men, but a movement consecrated to God. We are the remnant, gathered not by trend, but by call.”*

## **Digital Presence and Global Mission

The Christiana Brotherhood maintains an international digital presence, with spiritual reflections, alerts, visual symbolism, and direct messages to the faithful.

It offers content in three languages — **Portuguese**, **Spanish**, and **English** — reaching believers around the world and inviting them into a deeper alliance with the Kingdom.

Donations are accepted voluntarily to support publications, spiritual content, and assistance to brothers and sisters in need. All contributions are handled with transparency and prayer.

***

## **How to Join the Brotherhood

Those who feel the call are invited to explore the mission, read the spiritual statutes, and pray for discernment. The Brotherhood accepts individuals who:

- Confess personal faith in Jesus Christ

- Commit to daily spiritual discipline and truth

- Are willing to stand firm in holiness and brotherhood

- Desire to serve the light in a time of darkness

It is not an open community, but an alliance guided by prayer, vigilance, and spiritual purpose.
