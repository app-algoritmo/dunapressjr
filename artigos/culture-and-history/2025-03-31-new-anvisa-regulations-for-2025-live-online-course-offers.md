With the approval of RDC No. 907/2024, Brazil's National Health Surveillance Agency (ANVISA) has introduced a new regulatory framework that will come into full effect in 2025, bringing significant updates to the classification, registration, and post-market control of personal hygiene products, cosmetics, and perfumes.

To help professionals and companies navigate these changes, an **exclusive Live Online International Course** will be held on **May 17 and 24**, focusing on the **Regulatory Update for Cosmetic Product Registration – 2025**.

This intensive training offers **24 hours of live instruction**, aiming to **equip professionals and regulatory teams with essential knowledge** to remain compliant and competitive in the Brazilian market.

***

## 🔍 **What’s New in 2025? Understanding RDC No. 907/2024

The RDC No. 907/2024 modernizes Brazil’s regulatory approach and aligns it more closely with international practices. Key updates include:

- **Reclassification of product risk categories**, affecting whether a product requires prior registration or not.

- **Clearer definitions** for cosmetic products, their intended uses, and composition standards.

- **Stricter criteria for GMP (Good Manufacturing Practices)**, including documentation and traceability.

- **Enhanced cosmetovigilance protocols**, requiring manufacturers to monitor post-market safety and efficacy.

- **New standards for safety and efficacy evidence**, particularly for claims made in product labeling and advertising.

- **Updated requirements for stability studies** and **quality control** to ensure consistency throughout shelf life.

These changes mean that even companies familiar with ANVISA’s past regulations must **undergo updated training** to avoid non-compliance, product recalls, or legal challenges.
