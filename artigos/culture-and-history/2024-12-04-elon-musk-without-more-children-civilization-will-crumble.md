Elon Musk, the visionary behind companies like Tesla, SpaceX, and Neuralink, is known for his provocative statements. Recently, he once again raised an issue he considers crucial for humanity’s future: the birthrate crisis. In his words, *"So many people, including smart people, think there are too many people in the world and think the population is growing out of control. It's completely the opposite. Please look at the numbers. If people don't have more children, civilization is going to crumble."*

## **The Demographic Paradox

Despite the common perception that overpopulation is a global problem, recent data indicates that many countries are facing the opposite phenomenon: declining birthrates. Nations such as Japan, South Korea, and Italy are already grappling with demographic crises, with rapidly aging populations and fewer young people to sustain their economies and social systems.

Musk himself, a father of 11, often emphasizes that fertility rates below the replacement level (approximately 2.1 children per woman) could lead to the collapse of entire societies. In the long term, fewer births mean fewer people in the productive age group, which can negatively impact innovation, economies, and human progress.

## **The Numbers Don’t Lie

According to a UN report, over 60% of countries already have fertility rates below replacement level. China, for instance, recorded one of the most significant population declines in 2023, marking the start of a demographic decline expected to continue throughout the century. Other countries, such as Germany and Russia, face similar challenges.

Even in regions with historically growing populations, such as Sub-Saharan Africa, experts predict that increasing urbanization and education will also lead to a slowdown in population growth in the coming decades.

## **Why Does This Matter?

Musk argues that civilization, as we know it, depends on a growing population base to sustain economic, scientific, and technological development. Fewer people mean fewer minds to solve global problems and fewer workers to support social and technological advancements.

Additionally, he warns about the emotional and social impact of shrinking and aging populations, with elderly individuals facing loneliness and countries struggling with the rising costs of healthcare and pensions.

## **What Can Be Done?

For Musk, the solution is clear: people need to have more children. But this raises complex questions. How can societies encourage higher birthrates without compromising individual rights? Public policies that support families, such as extended parental leave, affordable childcare, and financial assistance, could be part of the solution.

However, Musk also points to a broader need for cultural change. In a world increasingly focused on careers and individual achievements, he believes it’s crucial to value and support family formation.

Elon Musk is not only an advocate for space exploration and artificial intelligence but is also deeply concerned about humanity's future here on Earth. His message about population decline is an urgent call for leaders and citizens to reconsider their priorities. After all, as he said, *"Without more children, civilization will crumble."*

The question now is: will the world listen?
