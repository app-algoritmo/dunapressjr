## Kvelde’s Vibrant May 17, 2025: Celebrating Norway’s National Day

On May 17, 2025, the charming village of Kvelde in [Larvik](https://www.larvik.kommune.no/kultur-idrett-og-frivillighet/arrangementer/17-mai/kvelde-17-mai-2025/), Norway, adorned itself in red, white, and blue to celebrate Norway’s National Day (Syttende Mai). This date, commemorating the signing of the Norwegian Constitution in 1814, symbolizes freedom, unity, and national identity. In Kvelde, the festivities were filled with tradition, music, and a strong sense of community.

## The Significance of May 17 in Norway

May 17 is more than a public holiday; it’s a vibrant expression of democratic pride and shared culture. Across Norway, children’s parades with flags, patriotic speeches, and choruses of “Ja, vi elsker” (the national anthem) fill the streets. In Kvelde, the day’s program embodied this spirit:

- Symbolic ceremonies: The day began at 8:00 AM with a flag-raising and wreath-laying at the war memorial (Krigsminnesmerke), honoring Norway’s history, with a speech by Olav Nordheim and music from Sigrid Hvitsten and Lågabrass.

- Community parade: The traditional tog (parade) started at 11:15 AM from Kvelde Church, winding through key locations like the nursing home, Futejordet, and Bygdetunet, with stops to sing “Ja, vi elsker” and “Norge i rødt, hvitt og blått”.

- Cultural events: From school performances to the heiarop (cheer competition) and the Øst og Vest duellen, the celebration strengthened intergenerational bonds.

***

## Kvelde: A Haven of Beauty and Heritage

Nestled in Larvik municipality, Vestfold county, Kvelde is a rural gem known for:

- Stunning nature: Surrounded by forests, lakes, and the Larvik fjord, it’s a haven for hiking and fishing.

- Rich heritage: The Bygdetunet (open-air museum) preserves local traditions, while Kvelde Church stands as a historical landmark.

- Tight-knit community: Events like May 17 highlight the cooperative spirit of residents, with activities such as the loddsalg (raffle sale) organized by 6th-grade students.

***

## A Day to Remember

With an inspiring speech by Kirvil Strand and music from Lågabrass, Kvelde’s May 17 celebration showcased how small communities keep national traditions alive. The organizing committee’s message, “Kjære bygda vår” (Dear community), invited everyone to join in.

Whether under the sun (with camping chairs) or in Kveldehallen (if raining), the spirit of unity shone through. For Norwegians, May 17 is a reminder that freedom and democracy are celebrated together—and Kvelde did so with joy and authenticity.

“Gratulerer med dagen!” (Happy National Day!)
