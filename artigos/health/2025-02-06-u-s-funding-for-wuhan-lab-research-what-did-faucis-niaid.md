A recently obtained document through a **Freedom of Information Act (FOIA)** request by *White Coat Waste Project* has revealed that the **National Institute of Allergy and Infectious Diseases (NIAID)**, formerly led by Dr. Anthony Fauci, and the **United States Agency for International Development (USAID)** allocated over **$40 million** in U.S. taxpayer funds to research at the **Wuhan Institute of Virology (WIV)** and related projects. The funding specifically targeted research into the **emergence of bat coronaviruses**—the same type of virus that led to the global COVID-19 pandemic.

## **Key Findings from the Documents

- **Investigator: Ben Hu**, a Chinese scientist at the Wuhan Institute of Virology, received funding to study **bat coronaviruses**.

- Two grants from **Fauci’s NIAID** provided **$5.58 million and $5.08 million**, respectively, for research at the **EcoHealth Alliance** and **WIV**.

- The **USAID** funneled **$38 million** into a project called **PREDICT-2**, subcontracted from **UC Davis** to **EcoHealth Alliance**.

- The **completion date** of these funding allocations? **2019**—the same year COVID-19 first emerged in Wuhan.

- Ben Hu was later **identified as "Patient Zero"** in multiple investigations into the origins of SARS-CoV-2.

## **Why Does This Matter?

These revelations raise **serious concerns** about:

1. **The U.S. government funding controversial "gain-of-function" research** on bat coronaviruses in Wuhan, despite concerns from experts about the risks of lab leaks.

2. **The timeline of funding and the outbreak of COVID-19**, which suggests that U.S. agencies may have unknowingly supported research that contributed to the pandemic.

3. **Dr. Anthony Fauci’s previous statements** in which he denied U.S. involvement in funding high-risk coronavirus research at the Wuhan Institute of Virology.
