## **Home Workouts to Boost Metabolism

## **Quick Training Routines to Stay in Shape

Staying active at home can be simpler than it seems—especially when your goal is to **boost metabolism** and **burn calories**. Combining **strength and cardio exercises** into short, intense sessions is an efficient strategy to improve physical conditioning, even with limited time.

In this article, we present a practical routine you can do at home, with no equipment needed, to fire up your metabolism and stay fit.

***

## **Why Combine Strength and Cardio?

The mix of **strength** (like squats and push-ups) and **cardio** (like jumping jacks and burpees) boosts calorie burning, improves endurance, and accelerates your metabolism—keeping your body active even after the workout (*afterburn effect*).

This approach also:

✔ **Saves time** – Short, intense workouts can be just as effective as longer sessions.

✔ **Preserves muscle mass** – Strength training helps prevent muscle loss during weight loss.

✔ **Improves conditioning** – Cardio strengthens your cardiovascular system.

***

## **20-Minute Metabolism-Boosting Routine

This sequence can be done at home, equipment-free, in a **circuit format**. Repeat the full cycle 3 to 4 times, resting 30 seconds between rounds.

### **1. Jump Squats (Cardio + Strength)** – 45 seconds

- Builds leg and glute strength while raising your heart rate.

### **2. Push-Ups (Strength)** – 45 seconds

- Works the chest, shoulders, and triceps.

### **3. Jumping Jacks (Cardio)** – 45 seconds

- Great for quickly revving up the metabolism.

### **4. Plank Shoulder Taps (Core Strength)** – 45 seconds

- Strengthens the core and improves stability.

### **5. Burpees (Total-Body Cardio)** – 45 seconds

- One of the best calorie-burning exercises out there.

***

## **Tips for Better Results

- **Keep the intensity up** – The faster (with good form), the higher the calorie burn.

- **Eat well** – Protein and whole carbs aid in muscle recovery.

- **Stay hydrated** – Water is essential for a healthy metabolism.

- **Rest well** – Good sleep enhances your results.

***

Short, high-intensity home workouts can be incredibly effective in **boosting metabolism** and **maintaining fitness**. By combining strength and cardio, you get double the benefit: **fat burning** and **muscle preservation**.

Try this routine 4 to 5 times per week and feel the difference in your **body and energy**! 💪
