The launch of DeepSeek has had a significant impact on the technology market. Companies such as Nvidia, Broadcom, and Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Company experienced notable stock declines, resulting in a total market capitalization loss of $1 trillion. Former U.S. President Donald Trump urged American tech companies to improve and compete with this groundbreaking innovation. ([as.com](https://as.com/actualidad/sociedad/china-revoluciona-la-ia-con-deepseek-y-trump-lanza-un-aviso-a-las-grandes-tecnologicas-de-eeuu-n/?utm_source=chatgpt.com))

DeepSeek’s open-source approach and focus on resource efficiency represent a significant shift in language model development, challenging industry norms and potentially democratizing access to advanced AI technologies.

## DeepSeek AI: Features, Pricing, Code, and Comparison with ChatGPT

DeepSeek is a Chinese artificial intelligence company that has gained prominence for developing open-source language models, offering performance comparable to leading market solutions but at significantly lower costs. Founded in 2023 by Liang Wenfeng, the company is headquartered in Hangzhou, Zhejiang, and is exclusively funded by the Chinese hedge fund High-Flyer. ([en.wikipedia.org](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DeepSeek?utm_source=chatgpt.com))

## **What is DeepSeek AI?

DeepSeek AI is an artificial intelligence-powered chatbot designed to interact with users similarly to ChatGPT. It can be used for various purposes, including answering questions, solving mathematical problems, assisting with programming, and generating textual content. ([bbc.com](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/c5yv5976z9po?utm_source=chatgpt.com))

## **Models and Technologies

DeepSeek has developed several language models, including DeepSeek-R1 and DeepSeek-V3.

- **DeepSeek-R1** provides responses comparable to contemporary models like OpenAI’s GPT-4o but was trained at a significantly lower cost—estimated at $6 million, compared to the $100 million spent on GPT-4 training in 2023. Additionally, DeepSeek-R1 requires only one-tenth of the computational power needed for a comparable language model. ([en.wikipedia.org](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DeepSeek?utm_source=chatgpt.com))

- **DeepSeek-V3** marks a significant improvement in inference speed compared to previous models, leading the ranking among open-source models. This model is freely available via web access, app, and API. ([deepseek.com](https://www.deepseek.com/?utm_source=chatgpt.com))

## **Pricing and Accessibility

One of the most notable features of DeepSeek is its accessibility. The company offers its AI assistant and underlying code for free, allowing developers and researchers to use and modify the technology as needed. ([theguardian.com](https://www.theguardian.com/business/2025/jan/27/tech-shares-asia-europe-fall-china-ai-deepseek?utm_source=chatgpt.com))

## **[Differences Between DeepSeek AI and ChatGPT](/deepseek-a-new-era-of-artificial-intelligence/)

- **Open Source vs. Closed Source**: While OpenAI's ChatGPT is a closed-source platform, DeepSeek adopts an open-source approach, making its code available for community use and modification. ([canaltech.com.br](https://canaltech.com.br/inteligencia-artificial/5-diferencas-entre-a-chinesa-deepseek-e-o-chatgpt/?utm_source=chatgpt.com))

- **Operational Cost**: DeepSeek was developed with a significantly lower investment compared to ChatGPT, making it a more cost-effective alternative. ([infomoney.com.br](https://www.infomoney.com.br/business/deepseek-versus-chatgpt-em-8-pontos-quais-sao-as-diferencas-segundo-a-ia/?utm_source=chatgpt.com))

- **Resource Efficiency**: By utilizing techniques such as "mixture of experts," DeepSeek activates only the computational resources necessary for each task, increasing efficiency and reducing energy consumption. ([theguardian.com](https://www.theguardian.com/world/2025/jan/28/tuesday-briefing-first-edition-donald-trump-gaza-proposal?utm_source=chatgpt.com))

- **Performance**: In specialized benchmarks, DeepSeek-R1 outperforms the GPT-4o1 model in areas like coding and logical progressions, while using only a fraction of its operational cost and computational resources.
